Flow styles come with **no moveset**. Instead, you work the style into your own **flow** for it to be effective. It's easily the **hardest** form of combat, needing patience and a level head, but a master of a Flow style is **extremely hard to beat**, even by someone stronger and faster.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Tai Chi">
    **Non-aggressive** tactics. Tai Chi users fight in a calm, zen-like state, and its true power is **restraint**: you know exactly where to strike, with **just the right amount of force** to take your opponent out.

    ### Flow State

    First, read your opponent's **physical flow** by **dodging** their attacks **3 times**. After that, you need **+3 extra** <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed to be Auto-Hit.

    :::caution
    An opponent a **Phase above you** in the S.T.S can still Auto-Hit you.
    :::

    ### Pressure Strike

    In the Flow State, every **6 turns**, roll **1d100**. A hit debuffs your opponent:

    | Roll | Strike | Effect |
    |--|--|--|
    | 1 – 50 | Nothing | — |
    | 51 – 60 | Arm Pressure Point | <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength **−¼** |
    | 61 – 70 | Double Arm Pressure Point | <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength **−½** |
    | 71 – 80 | Leg Pressure Point | <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed **−¼** |
    | 81 – 90 | Double Leg Pressure Point | <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed **−½** |
    | 91 – 99 | Full Body Strike | **Paralysis** for 2 turns |
    | 100 | Solar Plexus Strike | **Instant K.O.** |

    Two single Pressure Point hits on the same stat stack to **half**. Rolling **Nothing 3 times** puts Pressure Strike on a **6-turn cooldown**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Judo">
    Use your opponent's own speed **against them**. Judo shines against opponents **faster than you**.

    :::note[Special requirements]
    Your opponent's <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed must be **4 to 8 Sub-Tiers above yours**. Any higher or lower and Judo is unfit for the fight. Against an opponent in a **different Phase** of the S.T.S, Judo is **useless**.
    :::

    Take a **stance**. Your opponent must move in and strike you **physically**, then roll **1d10**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 4 | **Failure**: 5-turn cooldown |
    | 5 – 9 | **Success**: avoid the Auto-Hit with a counter, plus **1 free Non-Lethal hit** |
    | 10 | **Instant Pin**: overpower and pin them, then subdue them however you like |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Shaolin Kung Fu">
    An **Old World** style taught in dojos across the **Mainland** and **Outworld**. It balances defense **and** offense, and brings a crucial mechanic: **Parrying**.

    :::note[Special requirements]
    **Shave your head, and keep it shaved.**
    :::

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Way of the Monks" icon="information">
        In battle, your <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength and <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed **equalize** (the higher drops to the lower), then both drop **3 Sub-Tiers**.

        Once you **attack**, they return with **+1 Sub-Tier** for each hit you took while debuffed (**max +5**).
      </Card>
      <Card title="Parrying" icon="approve-check">
        Instantly deflect a **physical attack**. Roll **1d30**:

        | Roll | Result |
        |--|--|
        | 1 – 10 | Failure: **stunned** 1 turn |
        | 11 – 20 | **Parry**: deflect the attack |
        | 21 – 29 | **Critical Parry**: deflect + 1 free Auto-Hit |
        | 30 | **Super Critical**: deflect + **Instant K.O.** |
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Capoeira">
    A Brazilian art disguised as a **dance**. Capoeiristas never stand still: the constant sway of the **Ginga** makes them hard to pin down and builds into devastating spinning kicks.

    :::note[Special requirements]
    **Fight barefoot.** Your <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed must be **at least equal** to your <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength.
    :::

    ### Ginga

    Every attack you **dodge** builds **1 Momentum** (**max 5**). When you attack, spend Momentum: each point adds **+2 Power** to a kick. Getting hit **resets** your Momentum to 0.

    ### Meia Lua de Compasso

    At **5 Momentum**, spend it all on a spinning heel kick instead. Roll **1d20**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 7 | Whiffed: Momentum is lost |
    | 8 – 14 | **Sweep**: opponent is knocked down and **can't move** next turn |
    | 15 – 19 | **Clean Hit**: opponent is **stunned** 1 turn + 1 free Auto-Hit |
    | 20 | **Lights Out**: **Instant K.O.** |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Muay Thai">
    **The Art of Eight Limbs**: fists, elbows, knees and shins. Muay Thai fighters condition their bodies to **take punishment** and are at their deadliest **up close**.

    :::note[Special requirements]
    **Wrap your hands** before every fight. Your <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense must be **within 3 Sub-Tiers** of your <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength.
    :::

    ### Conditioning

    Every **3 physical hits** you take give **+1 Sub-Tier** of <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense for the rest of the fight (**max +4**).

    ### The Clinch

    Land **2 hits in a row** to lock your opponent in the clinch. While clinched, they **can't move away** or use **long-range** attacks. Each turn in the clinch, roll **1d12**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 4 | Opponent **breaks free** (clinch ends) |
    | 5 – 9 | **Knee**: **+4 Power** hit |
    | 10 – 11 | **Elbow Cut**: hit + opponent loses **5** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn for 3 turns |
    | 12 | **Knockout Knee**: opponent is **stunned** 2 turns |

    The clinch lasts **3 turns** at most, then needs a **5-turn cooldown**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
