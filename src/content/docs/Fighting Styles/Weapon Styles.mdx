Weapon styles are a **sub-class**: each one follows the rules of either the [Flow](/SingularityV5/fighting-styles/flow-styles/) or [Moveset](/SingularityV5/fighting-styles/moveset-styles/moveset-basics/) class. On top of that, the **type of weapon** gives a unique passive.

All three passives work the same way: after **10 regular attacks**, roll on your **11th**.

:::caution
Weapon passives **only** apply to **regular attacks**, not moves, and **don't apply** in Ultimate Mode.
:::

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Blunt">
    ## Blunt Weapons

    Bats, hammers: anything **without an edge**. Their weight makes every swing hit harder. Roll **1d20**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 4 | Nothing |
    | 5 – 10 | **Minor TBI**: opponent loses **5 Sub-Tiers** of <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed for 3 turns |
    | 11 – 15 | **Moderate Cranial Trauma**: opponent **can't use abilities** for 4 turns, and loses **5 Sub-Tiers** of <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed for 4 turns |
    | 16 – 19 | **You Good?**: opponent is **stunned** 1 turn, giving a free **Auto-Hit** |
    | 20 | **K.O.**: if they can't dodge, your **next hit** is an **Instant K.O.** |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Sharp">
    ## Sharp Weapons

    Swords, knives: anything **insanely sharp**. Used in swordsmanship styles, and closely tied to the **Outworld** style system: **Sword Styles**. Their passive is **Bleed**. Roll **1d20**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 4 | Nothing |
    | 5 – 10 | **Minor Bleed**: opponent loses **6** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn for 3 turns |
    | 11 – 15 | **Moderate Bleed**: **12** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn for 3 turns |
    | 16 – 19 | **Heavy Bleed**: **18** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn for 3 turns |
    | 20 | **Critical Bleed Strike**: opponent has **3 turns** before they're knocked out |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Ranged">
    ## Ranged Weapons

    Guns, bows and other projectile launchers, for styles that **keep their distance**. Their passive is **Ricochet**: roll **1d10** to see how many projectiles bounce back and hit your opponent **from behind**:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 1 – 3 | No success |
    | 4 – 6 | **Minor**: 1 projectile ricochets |
    | 7 – 8 | **Moderate**: 2 projectiles ricochet |
    | 9 | **Major**: 3 projectiles ricochet |
    | 10 | **Critical**: **all** projectiles ricochet |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
