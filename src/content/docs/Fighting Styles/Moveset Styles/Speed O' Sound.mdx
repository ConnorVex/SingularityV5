Quick movements with a **bladed weapon**. Unlike the brutish [Brawler](/SingularityV5/fighting-styles/moveset-styles/brawler/), Speed O' Sound is for people who can **really move**.

:::note[Special requirements]
<InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />**Speed:** 12F · **Height:** 5'9" · **Weapon:** Katana
:::

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Base: Speed O' Sound">
    **Passive: Chaining.** Use **2 attacks in one turn**. **3-turn cooldown** after use.

    | Move | Boost | Cooldown | Effect |
    |--|--|--|--|
    | **Quick Slice** | +5 Speed Sub-Tiers · Power **+6 per slice** | 3 turns, **+5 per extra slice** | Draw and unleash one slash, or a flurry |
    | **Circle of Encompassing Slashes** | +7 Speed Sub-Tiers | 7 turns | Vanish and strike from all sides. Roll **1d10** for the number of slashes. They must **dodge** you to avoid it |
    | **One Way** | +10 Speed Sub-Tiers | 5 turns | Leap off a surface and spin around them like a top, then strike after 1 turn. Great in a pinch |
    | **Afterimage Slice** | Counter | 7 turns | Hold a stance for **2 turns**. If they attack, they hit an **afterimage** and you get **1 free Auto-Hit** from behind |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Ultimate: Sonic Boom">
    The **perfected state** of the style: you embrace the speed, the sound, the world.

    ### Gaining Ultimate

    No gauge. Instead, land these during the fight:

    | Move | Requirement |
    |--|--|
    | Quick Slice | 3 successful hits |
    | Circle of Encompassing Slashes | 2 successful hits |
    | One Way | Chain into Quick Slice once **and** into COES once |
    | Afterimage Slice | 1 successful counter |

    Sonic Boom lasts **16 turns**.

        No Chaining cooldown: chain **any number** of moves in one post, as long as they're off cooldown.

    | **Two Way Bladed Revenge** | +14 Power | 8 turns | Slice their chest, zip back and slice their back. The mirage hits them **3 times** before knocking them away |
    | **X-Acto Knife, Fluctuation** | +12 Speed Sub-Tiers · +22 Power | Single use | **Only chained from Sonar Strike.** The vibrating blade cuts through higher Defense. Roll **1d20**: **10+** breaks your katana and **ends the Ultimate** |
    | **Sonar Strike** | +14 Speed Sub-Tiers · Power **15 + 5 per 4 Sub-Tiers** above your base Speed | 4 turns | Break the sound barrier and stop dead. The sonic boom **stuns** them: **1 free Auto-Hit** |

    ### You're So Slow, You Can't Even See Me

    Afterimages encircle your opponent. You both roll **1d20**:

    | Result | Effect |
    |--|--|
    | Tie, or you win by 1 | **Reroll** |
    | Opponent wins | They find you: you're knocked out of the counter and **stunned** 1 turn, and they can counter |
    | You win by 2 – 3 | 1 hit of **+4 Power** |
    | You win by 4 – 5 | 2 hits of **+8 Power** |
    | You win by 6 | 3 hits of **+12 Power** |
    | You win by 7 | 4 hits of **+16 Power** |
    | You win by 8+ | 5 hits of **+20 Power** |
    | You roll a **natural 20** | Every mirage hits: **Instant K.O.** |
