Infuse soap bubbles with **Hamon** to attack, defend and blind. **HP** = Hamon Points.

:::note[Requirements]
- [Hamon](/SingularityV5/power-systems/jojos-bizarre-adventure/hamon/hamon-basics/)
- A way to create bubbles you can infuse with Hamon
:::

| Skill | Cost | Stats | Effect |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Hamon Bubble Barrage** | 25 HP per bubble | Cooldown 3 turns · Speed **10C** · Power **18**, **+5 per extra 10 HP** | A barrage of bubbles. More bubbles, more damage |
| **Bubble Barrier** | 15 HP + **10 HP per 5 Defense Sub-Tiers**, per turn | Base Defense **5F** | A Hamon-infused barrier that withstands attacks |
| **Bubble Cutter** | 10 HP + 10 HP per extra bubble | **7** bubbles · Speed **10A** · Power **25 per bubble**, **+5 per 10 HP** added | Razor-sharp, durable bubbles. Each one counts as a [Sharp](/SingularityV5/fighting-styles/weapon-styles/) **and** [Ranged](/SingularityV5/fighting-styles/weapon-styles/) weapon, so use those rolls for each bubble |
| **Bubble Cutter Gliding** | 15 HP | **3** bubbles · Power **35 per bubble**, **+5 per 20 HP** added | Fewer, stronger bubbles you **steer yourself**: their Speed is **your** Speed |
| **Bubble Lenses** | 10 HP + 10 HP per extra bubble | **5** bubbles · Power **15**, **+7 per bubble** added · Speed of **light** | Stationary bubbles focus sunlight and Hamon into beams. They **burn**, and can **sever limbs** if Defense is too low |
