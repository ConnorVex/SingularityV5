**Zui Quan.** Stumble, sway and lurch like you've had one too many, and strike from angles nobody can read. Every fall is a feint.

:::note[Required stats]
<InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed at **Tier 4** and <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina at **Tier 5**.
:::

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Base: Drunken Fist">
    **Passive: Sway.** When a **physical attack** comes at you, roll **1d6**. On **5 – 6** you stumble out of the way and it **misses**. **3-turn cooldown** after a successful Sway.

    | Move | Boost | Cooldown | Effect |
    |--|--|--|--|
    | **Staggering Fist** | +8 Power | 5 turns | A wobbly punch from an impossible angle |
    | **Gourd Sweep** | +10 Power | 7 turns | Swing low with a gourd or bottle. Roll **1d10**: **6+** knocks them **down** for 1 turn |
    | **Tipsy Lean** | Counter | 6 turns | Fall backwards under their attack, then spring up with a **+12 Power** headbutt |
    | **Bottle Spray** | Debuff | 8 turns | Spray a mouthful in their eyes: **−4 Sub-Tiers** of <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed for 2 turns |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Ultimate: Eight Immortals">
    ### Gaining Ultimate

    Fill the **Ultimate Gauge (0/100)** with **UGP** from base moves:

    | Move | UGP per use |
    |--|--|
    | Staggering Fist | 6 |
    | Gourd Sweep | 10 |
    | Tipsy Lean | 15 (successful counters only) |
    | Bottle Spray | 8 |

    Eight Immortals lasts **8 turns**. When it ends, the **hangover** hits: **−6 Sub-Tiers** of <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed for **4 turns**.
