**Components** are the raw materials of crafting. You get them by completing **events** and **quests**, and sometimes you find them at random. The higher the tier, the rarer the component and the better the items it goes into.

Curse Essence is the exception: it can only be carried in a [Residue Containment Unit](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#residue-containment-unit).

## Mythic Components

Mythic components are **hard to get**, but the best items need them. Each one is obtained its own way.

### Singularity Residue

<Badge text="Unstable Energy Event" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="Containment Unit needed" variant="caution" />

The **Singularity Event** stretches forever, forwards and backwards through time. It was always happening and it never stops. Because of this paradox, a small piece of its energy **very rarely** breaks loose.

You can only get Singularity Residue during an **Unstable Energy Event**, and you need a [Residue Containment Unit](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#residue-containment-unit) to hold it.

:::caution[Unstable]
After **3 RPs** the containment unit fails and the energy dissipates. Use it before then.
:::

### Echo Drive

<Badge text="Fixer quests" variant="note" />

A drive with **near limitless storage**. Nobody knows how it does it. Echo Drives come from **quests for Fixers**, and they're rare and highly valuable.

*It is rumoured that an Echo Drive can store the consciousness of a person…*

### Calcified Speed Force

<Badge text="Speedsters only" variant="caution" />

A crystal holding a piece of the **Speed Force**, an extra-dimensional source of energy. It's used in recipes for certain **Speed Force** items.

Only a speedster who can **enter and exit the Speed Force** can get one, and even then there's only a small chance the energy **calcifies**. If it does, the crystal appears where the speedster last exited the Speed Force.

### Hardened Chronon Particle

<Badge text="Hardest to get" variant="danger" />

**Chronon energy** that has hardened into a shell around itself, like Calcified Speed Force. It packs enough energy to annihilate a massive area.

It's arguably the **most difficult component to get**: you need an abundant source of **Chronons** and a unique machine to harden the particle.

### Special Grade Curse Essence

<Badge text="Kill a Special Grade Curse" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="Containment Unit needed" variant="caution" />

Only dropped by **killing a Special Grade Curse**. It decays extremely fast, so it has to be extracted quickly into a [Residue Containment Unit](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#residue-containment-unit).
