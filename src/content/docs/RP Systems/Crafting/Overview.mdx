The **Crafting System** lets you gather items and smash them together into new ones. The items you make can give your character **extra capabilities**, and crafting can make you a bit of **money** to push your story forward.

## How Crafting Works

<Steps>

1. **Collect components.** You get them as rewards from **events** and **quests**, and sometimes you just **find** them. See [Components](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/components/).

2. **Pick a recipe** and check what it needs: its components, and the **Intelligence** level it asks for, if any. See [Recipes](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/recipes/).

3. **Craft it.** The components are used up, and the item is ready once its **crafting time** (real-life time) has passed.

4. **Keep it.** Using a crafted item **doesn't use it up**. It's yours until it's **destroyed**.

</Steps>

## Requirements

Every recipe lists what you need besides its components.

| Requirement | Who can craft it |
|--|--|
| <Badge text="No Unique Requirements" variant="success" /> | **Anyone** |
| <Badge text="Gifted" variant="note" /> | Gifted Intelligence **or higher** |
| <Badge text="Genius" variant="caution" /> | Genius Intelligence **or higher** |
| <Badge text="Super Genius" variant="danger" /> | Super Genius Intelligence |

Your Intelligence level is set on your character sheet. See [Intelligence Levels](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/stats-explained/#intelligence-levels).

## Building Blocks

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Components" icon="puzzle">
    The raw materials, in five tiers from **Common** to **Mythic**. Mythic components each have their own **unique way** to get them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crafted Components" icon="setting">
    Parts you **craft from components** and then use in bigger recipes, like the **Residue Containment Unit** that stores unstable energy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Special Items" icon="star">
    Some recipes also need items that **can't be crafted**, like **Velocity-X** or **Blast Shards**. You get these from **shops** and **special drops**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Items" icon="pencil">
    Want something that isn't a recipe yet, like a **suit** or a **weapon**? Send an **Equipment Submission** and a Gamemaster will tell you exactly what it takes to craft it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
