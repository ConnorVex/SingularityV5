**Crafted Components** are parts you **craft** instead of collect. You use them in recipes for bigger items, and some of them unlock components you couldn't hold otherwise. Like components, they come in tiers: the higher the tier, the harder they are to make.

| Part | Rarity | Requires |
|--|--|--|
| [Chemical Refinery](#chemical-refinery) | Common | None |
| [Corrosion Resistant Injector](#corrosion-resistant-injector) | Rare | Gifted |
| [Residue Containment Unit](#residue-containment-unit) | Legendary | Genius |
| [Cellular Matrix Stabilizer](#cellular-matrix-stabilizer) | Mythic | Genius |
| [Echo Helmet Interface](#echo-helmet-interface) | Mythic | Genius |
| [RELIC](#relic) | Mythic | Genius |

Each part lists its **crafting time** (real-life time) below.

|--|--|

| Grade 3 Curse Essence | 2 |

| Special Grade Curse Essence | 50 |
