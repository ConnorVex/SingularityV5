## Common

### Enhanced VX

<Badge text="Common" /> <Badge text="No Unique Requirements" variant="success" /> <Badge text="10 minutes" variant="note" />

An enhanced **Velocity-X**. Artificial speedsters get faster, the boost for natural speedsters is stronger, and it lasts longer.

**Needs:**
- 1× Velocity-X <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 1× [Chemical Refinery](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#chemical-refinery)
- 10× Chemical Manipulator

| Upgrade | Velocity-X | Enhanced VX |
|--|--|--|
| Artificial speedster | Tier 10F | Tier 12A (+15 Speed if already above 12A) |
| Speedster boost | 10 sub-tiers | 15 sub-tiers |
| Duration | 10 turns | 15 turns |

### Enhanced Quirk Amplifier

<Badge text="Common" /> <Badge text="No Unique Requirements" variant="success" /> <Badge text="5 minutes" variant="note" />

An upgrade to the regular **Quirk Amplifier Device**: a bigger boost and a longer charge, in exchange for a much longer recharge. A **must** if you want to take amplification to the next level.

**Needs:**
- 1× Quirk Amplifier Device <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 10× Copper Wires
- 10× Circuit Boards

| Upgrade | Q.A.D | Enhanced |
|--|--|--|
| Quirk Power | Ultra-Amplified (110) (18B) | Apex Quirk (135) (21A) |
| Device charge | 6 turns | 12 turns |
| Recharge time | 2 RPs | 4 RPs |

### Rudimentary Blast Energy Locator

<Badge text="Common" /> <Badge text="No Unique Requirements" variant="success" /> <Badge text="10 minutes" variant="note" />

Reveals every **Blast Shard** within about **4 channels**. It's **single use**: it fails for good after one pulse.

**Needs:**
- 10× Copper Wires
- 10× Circuit Boards
- 10× Blast Shards <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />

## Rare

### Meta Power Dampener

<Badge text="Rare" variant="note" /> <Badge text="Gifted" variant="note" /> <Badge text="10 minutes" variant="note" />

Disables the powers of anyone caught in it. Choose the version when you craft it: the **Physical** version has to be placed on the person you want to depower, while the **A.O.E** version is deployed and hits everyone in the channel.

**Needs:**
- 10× Alloys
- 5× Harmonic Core
- 5× Disruption Ink Cartridge
- **Physical:** 20× Adaptive Alloy Sample
- **A.O.E:** 20× Psi-Echo Crystal

| Version | Disables | Lasts |
|--|--|--|
| Physical | Quirks, Abilities, Pyrokinetics, Cursed Energy and Cursed Techniques | 4 turns |
| A.O.E | Quirks and Abilities | 6 turns |

### Enhanced Blast Energy Locator

<Badge text="Rare" variant="note" /> <Badge text="Gifted" variant="note" /> <Badge text="10 minutes" variant="note" />

Finds **Blast Shards and Blast Cores** across an **entire District**. Like the Rudimentary version, it's **single use**.

**Needs:**
- 1× [Rudimentary Blast Energy Locator](#rudimentary-blast-energy-locator)
- 20× Copper Wires
- 20× Circuit Boards
- 10× Blast Shards <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 1× Blast Core <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />

### Overcharged Trigger

<Badge text="Rare" variant="note" /> <Badge text="Gifted" variant="note" /> <Badge text="1 day" variant="note" />

A corrected **Trigger** formula: it goes much higher than the normal drug, and its side effects are weaker.

**Needs:**
- 1× Trigger Drug <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 1× [Corrosion Resistant Injector](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#corrosion-resistant-injector)
- 20× Chemical Processor
- 20× Electrochemical Array
- 5× [Chemical Refinery](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#chemical-refinery)

| Upgrade | Trigger | Overcharged |
|--|--|--|
| Quirk Power | Amplified (95) (16E) | Overcharged (115) (19C) |
| Duration | 1 RP | 3 RPs |
| Trigger Skills | — | +4 Standard, +4 Ultimate |
| Weakness | 4 sub-tiers for 3 RPs | 2 sub-tiers for 1 RP |
| Quirk Loss | 4 RPs | 2 RPs |

## Legendary

### Velocity XI

<Badge text="Legendary" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="Gifted" variant="note" /> <Badge text="2 days" variant="note" />

The upgrade to **Velocity-X**. It makes you an **artificial speedster**, much faster and for much longer, but it has a nasty side effect if you use it too much.

**Needs:**
- 3× Velocity-X <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 10× [Chemical Refinery](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#chemical-refinery)
- 20× Chemical Manipulator
- 15× Chemical Processor
- 1× [Corrosion Resistant Injector](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#corrosion-resistant-injector)

| Upgrade | Velocity-X | Velocity XI |
|--|--|--|
| Artificial speedster | Tier 10F | Tier 16F (+20 Speed if already above 12A) |
| Speedster boost | 10 sub-tiers | 20 sub-tiers |
| Duration | 10 turns | 2 RPs |

:::danger[Cellular Degeneration]
Use Velocity XI **3–4 times** and your cells start to degenerate. Once it sets in, **nothing stops it**: you gain the **Terminal** trait.
:::

### Velocity XII

<Badge text="Legendary" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="Genius" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="2 days" variant="note" />

The **final form** of the Velocity series. The speed boost over Velocity XI is small; what makes it special is the **Cellular Matrix Stabilizer**, which makes it last far longer and removes **addiction and Cellular Degeneration** completely.

**Needs:**
- 5× Velocity-X <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 1× [Enhanced VX](#enhanced-vx)
- 2× [Velocity XI](#velocity-xi)
- 20× Chemical Manipulator
- 20× [Chemical Refinery](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#chemical-refinery)
- 50× Chemical Processor
- 1× [Corrosion Resistant Injector](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#corrosion-resistant-injector)
- 1× [Cellular Matrix Stabilizer](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#cellular-matrix-stabilizer)

| Upgrade | Velocity-X | Velocity XII |
|--|--|--|
| Artificial speedster | Tier 10F | Tier 18A (+25 Speed if already above 12A) |
| Speedster boost | 10 sub-tiers | 25 sub-tiers |
| Duration | 10 turns | 5 RPs |

### Perfected Blast Energy Locator

<Badge text="Legendary" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="Genius" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="1 day" variant="note" />

The final version of the locator. It covers an **entire Zone** and finds **Blast Shards, Blast Cores and Core Relays**. It doesn't burn out: **recharge** it with Blast energy after each pulse.

**Needs:**
- 2× [Rudimentary Blast Energy Locator](#rudimentary-blast-energy-locator)
- 1× [Enhanced Blast Energy Locator](#enhanced-blast-energy-locator)
- 30× Copper Wires
- 10× EMP-Stabilized Conductor
- 20× Blast Shards <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- 5× Blast Core <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />

| One recharge costs | |
|--|--|
| Blast Shards | 100 |
| *or* Blast Cores | 50 |
| *or* Core Relays | 1 |

## Mythic

### Special Grade Cursed Tool

<Badge text="Mythic" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="No Unique Requirements" variant="success" /> <Badge text="1 week" variant="note" />

A tool with a **Cursed Technique engraved** into it, for niche situations. The Curse Essence it needs depends on the kind of technique you engrave.

**Needs:**
- 1× Grade 1 Cursed Tool <Badge text="Special" variant="tip" />
- Curse Essence, depending on the kind of technique:

| Technique | Curse Essence |
|--|--|
| **Offensive** | 20× Grade 4<br />10× Grade 3<br />5× Grade 2<br />1× Grade 1 |
| **Defensive** | 10× Grade 4<br />15× Grade 3<br />1× Grade 2<br />1× Grade 1 |
| **Technique Dampener** | 50× Grade 4<br />20× Grade 3<br />10× Grade 2<br />10× Grade 1<br />3× Special Grade |

### Hollow Echo Drive

<Badge text="Mythic" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="Genius" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="1 week" variant="note" />

A helmet that copies a person's consciousness into an **engram** saved on a chip. Putting it on someone takes **3 turns**, and it must not be interrupted. The engram lasts **5 RPs** before it dissipates.

You can talk to the engram like a normal person, or use it to **predict** that person's moves: you **auto-dodge** their attacks.

**Needs:**
- 1× Echo Drive
- 1× [RELIC](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#relic)
- 1× [Echo Helmet Interface](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#echo-helmet-interface)
- 10× Adaptive Circuits
- 20× Flux Capacitors
- 20× EMP-Stabilized Conductor

### Speed Force Extractor

<Badge text="Mythic" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="Genius" variant="caution" /> <Badge text="5 days" variant="note" />

Takes the **Speed Force** from a natural speedster, to store it or give it to someone else. A non-speedster can gain the Speed Force **without the Velocity series**, or a speedster can **add the stolen speed** to their own.

**Needs:**
- 1× [Corrosion Resistant Injector](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#corrosion-resistant-injector)
- 1× Calcified Speed Force
- 1× [Cellular Matrix Stabilizer](/SingularityV5/rp-systems/crafting/crafted-components/#cellular-matrix-stabilizer)
- 100× Adaptive Circuits
- 100× Corrosion Resistant Alloys
- 50× Phase Dust Vials
- 20× Flux Capacitors

### Chronon Detector

<Badge text="Mythic" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="Super Genius" variant="danger" /> <Badge text="1 week" variant="note" />

Tells you when a **Chronon Anomaly** or a **Chronon Active Individual (C.A.I)** is near, by watching the energy of its Hardened Chronon Particle: if it rises, one is close. It has no combat use, but a smart user can find places to **harvest Chronon energy** as a power source.

**Needs:**
- 1× Hardened Chronon Particle
- 1× [Perfected Blast Energy Locator](#perfected-blast-energy-locator)
- 500× Copper Wires
- 200× Adaptive Circuits
- 200× Flux Capacitors
- 50× Phase Dust Vials
- 10× NanoFlex Alloy Mk.III
