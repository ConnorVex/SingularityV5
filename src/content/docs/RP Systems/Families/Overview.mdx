Most characters start out on their own, but some are accepted into a **Family**. Families matter a lot in the world of Singularity: some are just a small group of people, while others **shape the way you roleplay**.

Families can also offer **perks you can't get any other way**. Some powers are even **locked behind** certain families.

## Key Points

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Player Run" icon="star">
    Every family is **run by a player**, its **Owner**. There are no NPC families. The owner decides who joins and how the family is organised.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Join Any Number" icon="add-document">
    A character can belong to **as many families as they like**, as long as each family accepts them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Perks" icon="rocket">
    Families can offer **Family traits**, **Family Locked powers** and other perks. Every perk is **approved by the admins**, and some families keep theirs **secret** from outsiders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Relations" icon="comment">
    Families have standings with each other, from **Allies** to **Mortal Enemies**. Your family's standing changes how its members treat you.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Joining a Family

<Steps>

1. **Find a family** in the [Family Directory](/SingularityV5/families/). Check whether it's **recruiting**, and whether it holds **tryouts**.

2. **Ask to join** from the family's page with an approved character. If the family holds **tryouts**, press **Request a tryout** first. A tryout is a **sample** of a brand-new character made for the family. It has no stats, only a name, who they are, an optional backstory, and a short piece of RP written as them. Only you, the owner and admins can read it.
   - If the owner **accepts** it, make that character in the [portal](/SingularityV5/portal/submit/), get it approved, then come back and ask to join with it.

3. **Get accepted.** Once the owner accepts, your character is on the family's **roster**, and you can use the family's perks.

</Steps>

:::caution[Leaving a family]
If your character **leaves** a family or is **kicked out**, they're **removed from its roster** completely, and they lose that family's perks.
:::

## How a Family Is Run

| Role | Who | What they do |
|--|--|--|
| **Owner** | A player | Runs the family: accepts and removes members, edits the family's page |
| **Head of Family** | A character | Leads the family in roleplay |
| **Members** | Characters | Belong to the family, often inside a **division** |

Big families split into **divisions** with their own leaders and purpose. The Brando Clan calls them **Phalanxes**.

## Perks

A perk can be **anything**, and every one is approved **case by case** by the admins. Not every family needs to be a powerhouse: a small family might just offer a safehouse and some starting money.

| Kind | Small example | Big example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starting stats** | +1 Sub-Tier in Speed | Higher starting stats and extra Tier Points |
| **Family trait** | A trait that runs in the family | Several exclusive traits to pick from |
| **Trait slots** | 1 extra slot | A different trait-slot setup |
| **Power system start** | A starting level in a power system | A full starting package |
| **Family Locked power** | — | A technique only the family holds |
| **Secret technique** | A family move | A transformation only the family has mastered |
| **Fighting style** | A style taught to members | Several exclusive styles |
| **Items & vault** | Starting gear | A shared vault of rare items |
| **Better rolls** | One extra roll | Picking instead of rolling |
| **Faster growth** | Quicker training | Faster evolutions and mastery |
| **Money** | Starting money | Better earnings |
| **Standing & protection** | A safehouse or contacts | Protection across a Zone |

Each perk also says **who gets it**: every member, members who **pick** it, members who **roll** for it, members who **earn** it in RP, or members **taught** it by another member.

The owner sends perks from **Manage family → Perks**, as many as they like in **one request**. The admins approve or deny **each perk on its own**, so a request of 5 might come back with 4 approved. Changing a perk sends it back for approval; removing one doesn't need approval.

A Family trait takes **1 trait slot** whatever its rarity, and a character can have **only 1**, unless one of the family's perks says otherwise.

<Aside type="note" title="Members-only perks">
A family can hide a perk from everyone outside it. Outsiders only see a **locked** card until their character joins. The admins approve hiding a perk along with the perk itself.
</Aside>

A perk only counts once your character has been **accepted** into the family. They lose it if they leave or are kicked out.
