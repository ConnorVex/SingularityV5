Anyone can found a family, but every new family is **approved by the admins** first. Each player can **own 1 family**.

## How to Found One

<Steps>

1. **Plan your family.** Work out who they are, where they come from and what they're known for.

2. **Submit it to the admins** on the [Found a Family](/SingularityV5/families/new/) page, with everything under [What to Submit](#what-to-submit).

3. **Wait for approval.** The admins may ask for changes. Perks are agreed **case by case**.

4. **Build your roster.** Once approved, your family appears in the [Family Directory](/SingularityV5/families/) and characters can ask to join. You edit your page, roster, relations and perks from **Manage family** on that page.

</Steps>

## What to Submit

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Required" icon="approve-check">
    - **Name**
    - **Head of Family:** the character who leads it
    - **Description:** a short summary of the family
    - **Lore:** where they come from and what they've done
    - **Race** and **Specialty**, like *Sorcery* or *Heroism*
    - **Mainland**, **Outworld**, or both
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional" icon="pencil">
    - **Banner** image and a **colour**
    - **Divisions:** groups inside the family, each with a name, purpose and leader
    - **Home:** where the family lives
    - **Sister families** and starting **relations**
    - **Perks** you'd like to offer, and which ones are **members only**
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What Owners Can Change

After approval, the owner can update most of the family's page themselves. Anything that changes a **character sheet** goes back to the admins.

| Change | Approval |
|--|--|
| Description, lore, banner, colour | <Badge text="Owner" variant="success" /> |
| Divisions, titles, open spots | <Badge text="Owner" variant="success" /> |
| Members: accept, remove | <Badge text="Owner" variant="success" /> |
| Relations and sister families | <Badge text="Owner" variant="success" /> |
| Recruiting and tryouts | <Badge text="Owner" variant="success" /> |
| Perks, sent together and approved one by one | <Badge text="Admins" variant="caution" /> |
| Name | <Badge text="Admins" variant="caution" /> |
| Special status, like **Monarch Family** | <Badge text="Admins" variant="caution" /> |
| A new owner | <Badge text="Admins" variant="caution" /> |
