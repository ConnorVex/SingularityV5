Every family has a **standing** with the other families. The owner sets it, and it tells members how to treat people from that family.

## Standings

| Standing | What it means |
|--|--|
| <Badge text="Allies" variant="success" /> | Close partners. Allies often get access other outsiders don't |
| <Badge text="Friendly" variant="tip" /> | On good terms. Expect protection and help |
| <Badge text="Neutral" /> | No strong feelings either way |
| <Badge text="Uneasy" variant="note" /> | Tense. Members keep their distance |
| <Badge text="Hostile" variant="caution" /> | Openly against each other |
| <Badge text="Mortal Enemies" variant="danger" /> | No talks, no peace |

Each family decides what its standings mean in practice. For example, the [Brando Clan](/SingularityV5/families/view/?f=brando-clan) lets Allies into the lobby of its High-rises.

## Improving a Standing

Any family can ask for a **diplomatic meeting** to improve its standing with another family. The one exception is **Mortal Enemies**: they won't meet, so don't let it get that far.

## Sister Families

A **sister family** has a bond so strong that both are considered **family**. They're often related by blood or history, and members of a sister family are usually under the other family's **protection**.
