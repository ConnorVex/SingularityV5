Being born with an ability doesn't mean you have the **skill** to use it. The **Skill System** sets what you can do with your ability (or **abilities**), what each move costs, and how you get better at them over time.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Skills cost Stamina" icon="rocket">
    Every skill costs a **percentage** of your **max** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina, so costs **scale with you** from Tier 1 to Tier 20.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Practice pays off" icon="star">
    The more you use a skill, the more you **master** it. Mastered skills cost **less** and unlock a **perk**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

:::note[Systems come first]
A Power System or Fighting Style can set its **own** costs or rules for its skills (like Hamon's **Hamon Points**). When it does, **follow the system**.
:::
