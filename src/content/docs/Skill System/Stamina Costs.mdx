Skills cost a **percentage of your max** <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina (the Stamina value of your current [S.T.S rank](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phases-overview/)). **Round up**, so every skill costs at least **1**.

| Skill | Cost |
|--|--|
| <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | **10%** of max Stamina |
| <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | **30%** of max Stamina |

[Skill Mastery](/SingularityV5/skill-system/skill-mastery/) lowers these costs.

## Examples

| Rank | Max Stamina | Standard | Ultimate |
|--|--|--|--|
| **2F** | 20 | 2 | 6 |
| **4A** | 100 | 10 | 30 |
| **6C** | 151 | 16 | 46 |
| **10C** | 400 | 40 | 120 |

## Running Low

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Winded" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="Below 25%" variant="caution" />

    You're running on fumes: **Ultimate** skills are **locked** until you recover above 25%.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exhausted" icon="error">
    <Badge text="0 Stamina" variant="danger" />

    You **can't use skills** at all, only basic actions like moving and regular attacks.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

:::danger[Overexertion]
You can force out **one** skill you can't afford. It works, but once it resolves you **collapse** and are **knocked out**.
:::

## Recovering

<Steps>

1. **Catch your breath:** spend a turn without using skills or attacking to recover **10%** of your max Stamina.

2. **After the fight:** a proper rest out of combat restores you to **full**.

</Steps>

## Multiple Power Sets

If you have more than one power set, your **non-priority** systems pay **double** Stamina for their Standard and Ultimate skills. See the [Priority System](/SingularityV5/important-systems/priority-system/).
