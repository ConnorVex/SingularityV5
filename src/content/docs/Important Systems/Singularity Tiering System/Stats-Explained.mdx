Six stats describe your character's body. **Strength**, **Speed**, **Defense** and **Stamina** are ranked in tiers. **Power** is worked out from them, and **Intelligence** has its own levels.

<CardGrid>
  <StatCard stat="strength">
    Your **lifting strength**: what you can lift over your head, from a coffee mug to a CAT 798 mining truck.
  </StatCard>
  <StatCard stat="speed">
    Covers two things:
    - **Movement speed:** how fast you move.
    - **Reflexes:** what you can and can't react to.
  </StatCard>
  <StatCard stat="power">
    **How hard you hit.** Unlike the other stats it isn't a tier, but a number:

    <p class="formula">Power = (Strength + Speed) ÷ 2</p>

    Use the **Value** of your Strength and Speed ranks, listed in the [Phase tables](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phases-overview/).
  </StatCard>
  <StatCard stat="defense">
    **How durable you are.** Each Defense rank has a Value: the **Power** needed to hurt you.
  </StatCard>
  <StatCard stat="stamina">
    **Every task uses Stamina**, so manage it carefully. Run out, and bad things can and will happen. Skills cost Stamina too: see [Stamina Costs](/SingularityV5/skill-system/stamina-costs/).
  </StatCard>
  <StatCard stat="intelligence">
    How easily your character **understands concepts**. A higher level doesn't make you a know-it-all, but it does make new ideas easier to grasp.
  </StatCard>
</CardGrid>

## Intelligence Levels

Everyone starts at **Average**. You can trade your **starting** PTP to move up, or move down to gain PTP. Each step costs or gives **10 PTP**.

| Level | Starting PTP |
|--|--|
| Below Average | **+10** |
| Average | — |
| Above Average | **−10** |
| Gifted | **−20** |
| Genius | **−30** |
| Super Genius | <Badge text="Permission Only" variant="caution" /> |
