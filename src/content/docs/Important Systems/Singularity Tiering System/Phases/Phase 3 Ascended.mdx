The **final Phase** for almost everyone: you're crossing into **territory where legends live**. It's split into **3 Sub-Phases**, each needing its own Potential.

| Sub-Phase | Tiers | Potential required |
|--|--|--|
| **Sub-Phase 1** | 15 – 16 | <Badge text="Tremendous+" variant="caution" /> |
| **Sub-Phase 2** | 17 – 18 | <Badge text="Apex" variant="danger" /> |
| **Sub-Phase 3** | 19 – 20 | <Badge text="Unparalleled" variant="danger" /> |

*Strength in KT = kilotons lifted. Defense in kt / Mt TNT = kilotons / megatons of TNT withstood.*
