The S.T.S is split into **Phases**. Each Phase unlocks **new stat tiers** to reach, with its own **physical evolution** requirements.

:::caution[Potential sets your limit]
Your **Potential** trait decides how far you can go. The higher your Potential, the further you can climb.
:::

## The Phases

| Phase | Tiers | Potential required | Hop to next Phase |
|--|--|--|--|
| [**Phase 0: Base Human**](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phase-0-base-human/) | 1 – 2 | Any | 15 PTP |
| [**Phase 1: Inhuman**](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phase-1-inhuman/) | 3 – 8 | Low → Average | 25 PTP |
| [**Phase 2: Superhuman**](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phase-2-superhuman/) | 9 – 14 | Great → Colossal | 30 PTP |
| [**Phase 3: Ascended**](/SingularityV5/important-systems/singularity-tiering-system/phases/phase-3-ascended/) | 15 – 20 | Tremendous+ | <Badge text="Final for almost everyone" variant="caution" /> |
| **Phase Ω: Divine** | — | <Badge text="Divine" variant="danger" /> | — |

## Physical Training

Training earns **Physical Points**, which fill your current Phase's **meter**. A full meter gives you **15 Physical Tiering Points (PTP)** to spend on your stats.

| Phase | Meter |
|--|--|
| Base Human | 0/25 |
| Inhuman | 0/35 |
| Superhuman | 0/45 |
| Ascended | 0/55 |
