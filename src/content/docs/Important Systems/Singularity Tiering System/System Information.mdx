The **Singularity Tiering System (S.T.S)** is Singularity's stat system. It measures what your character can physically do at any given time, and what they can't.

## How It Works

<Steps>

1. **Spend your points.** You get **Physical Tiering Points (PTP)** to put into your stats. You choose where they go, so spend them wisely.

2. **Train.** Training fills your Phase's meter. A full meter gives you **15 PTP**.

3. **Stay balanced.** Keep Strength, Speed and Defense close together, or you'll suffer **Recoil**.

4. **Hop Phases.** Max out your stats and have the **Potential** for it, and you can move up to the next Phase.

</Steps>
