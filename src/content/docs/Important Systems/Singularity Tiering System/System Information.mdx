<u>The Singularity Tiering System, or S.T.S is then custom stat system that is used to gauge the physical capabilities of a character at any given time.</u> It is a Stat based System that offers a more in depth look of the capabilities of a character, and will give you further input on what you can and cannot do.

You have full control of where you place your points, just make sure to do it wisely.
