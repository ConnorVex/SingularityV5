## <InlineIcon name="hazards/recoil" /> Recoil

Having too much power without the body to handle it causes **Recoil**. Keep your stats balanced and you'll be fine.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The Rule">
    Your <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />**Strength**, <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />**Speed** and <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />**Defense** must all stay within **2 Sub-Tiers** of each other.

    - Too much Defense: you can't lift yourself.
    - Too much Speed: you become a human fireball.
    - Too much Strength: you rip your arms off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Examples">
    <div class="hazard-table">

    | | <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" /> | <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" /> | <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" /> |
    |--|:--:|:--:|:--:|
    | ✅ **Valid** | 7A | 8F | 8E |
    | ❌ **Recoil** | 8D | 6E | 3B |

    </div>
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## <InlineIcon name="hazards/threshold" /> Physical Threshold

Every body has a limit to how far it can be pushed. This is your **Physical Threshold**, also called your **Limiter**.

:::note[Potential Traits]
Your Physical Threshold is set by your **Potential Traits**. The higher your Potential, the higher the S.T.S Phases you can reach before you're stopped.
:::
