If you start your journey with **more than 1 power set**, you have to choose which power system to **prioritize**. You can only have **one** priority system at a time.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Priority System" icon="star">
    Works **as normal**, with no buffs or debuffs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Non-Priority Systems" icon="warning">
    Harder to use: the <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina cost of their **Standard and Ultimate** skills is **doubled**. Custom Stamina values are doubled too.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Changing Priority

<Steps>

1. **Fully master** your priority system.

2. Choose **another system** to prioritize.

3. Your mastered system and your new priority system now **both** work **without drawbacks**.

</Steps>
