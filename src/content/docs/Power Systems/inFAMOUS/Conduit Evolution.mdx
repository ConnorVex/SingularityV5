:::caution[Training doesn't evolve Conduits]
**No amount of training or ability use** evolves Conduit powers. You need **evolution items** to progress your class's Upgrade Tree.
:::

## Evolution Items

| Item | Rarity | What it is | Notes |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Blast Shard** | Common | Energy from a pre-Singularity world, scattered across Terra (some **grow out of plants**) | Any Conduit can absorb it. **Sells for a lot** |
| **Blast Core** | <Badge text="Very rare" variant="caution" /> | **Hardened** Blast Shard energy with far more power | Unlocks bigger evolution paths. Key part of the D.U.P's Core Relays. **50 Cores = 1 Core Relay** |
| **Core Relay** | <Badge text="Rarest" variant="danger" /> | **Conduit Identification Units (C.I.U.)**: D.U.P devices that track and catalogue Conduits | Even more potent than Cores. **Mainland only** |

:::danger[Core Relays]
**Absorbing a Core Relay can attract unwanted attention. You have been warned!**
:::

Only Conduits have **ARC Sense**, so only they can **find** these items.

## Arc Rolls

Roll `/roll 1d1000` to search for items:

| Roll | Find |
|--|--|
| 0 – 499 | Nothing |
| 500 – 790 | Blast Shard |
| 791 – 899 | Blast Core |
| 900+ | Core Relay *(Mainland only)* |

- **3 rolls per location** per RP, with a **5-turn cooldown** between rolls.
- **3 rolls with nothing?** You can't roll in that channel again for **4 RPs** (with others).

## Gaining New Moves

New moves need **Core Relays**. Unlocked skills are **available immediately**.

| Core Relays absorbed | Gain |
|--|--|
| Every **1** | 1 **Standard** skill |
| Every **2** | 1 **Special** (Ultimate move) |

- **Elemental, Energy, Meta and Quantum:** **no limit**, as long as you keep absorbing Core Relays.
- **Psychic and Bio-Enhanced:** **can't** develop new skills.

## Karma Bomb

An **ultra-powerful single move** that can overwhelm up to **21A Defense**.

  <Card title="How it works" icon="rocket">
