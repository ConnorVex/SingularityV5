A Conduit's **actions and state of mind** shape their powers. You walk the **Path of Selflessness** or the **Path of Selfishness**. The only variable? **You.**

## The Karma Bar

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="How it fills" icon="random">
    - Starts at **0**. Fill it with **Positive (PK)** or **Negative (NK)** Karma Points.
    - First rank needs **100**, and every rank after needs **+30 more**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Going against your path" icon="warning">
    Once you lean one way, actions from the **other** side **subtract** from your bar.

    *Example:* 50 PK, then you kill a civilian (10 NK), leaves you at **40 PK**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## The Two Paths

| | Selflessness (Good) | Selfishness (Evil) |
|--|--|--|
| **Mindset** | Use your powers to **help others** | Humans are **inferior**: use your powers for **yourself** |
| **Ranks** | Protector → Guardian → Champion → Paragon → **True Hero** | Thug → Criminal → Bio-Terrorist → Most Wanted → **Infamous** |
| **Powers grow toward** | **Incapacitating** abilities | **Lethal** abilities |
| **You lose** | **All lethality** by **Champion** | **All incapacitation** by **Bio-Terrorist** |

## Earning Karma

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Positive Karma (PK)" icon="approve-check">
    | Action | PK |
    |--|--|
    | Rescue or heal a civilian | 5 |
    | Stop a crime | 6 |
    | Incapacitate a villain *(OC only)* | 10 |
    | Incapacitate a **surrendering** villain *(OC only)* | 20 |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Negative Karma (NK)" icon="error">
    | Action | NK |
    |--|--|
    | Kill a civilian | 10 |
    | Commit a crime | 12 |
    | Kill a hero/vigilante *(OC only)* | 20 |
    | Kill a **surrendering** hero/vigilante *(OC only)* | 40 |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
