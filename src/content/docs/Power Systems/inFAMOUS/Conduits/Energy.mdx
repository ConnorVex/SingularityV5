One of the **rarest** classes, and **deadlier** than Elementals. Energy Conduits control **energy fields** like gamma rays, neutrons, photons and even **anti-matter**.

| | Elemental | Energy |
|--|--|--|
| Fuel | **Absorbs** its element | **Generates** its own energy |
| Become your power | <Badge text="Yes" variant="success" /> | <Badge text="No" variant="danger" /> |

## Starting Stats

```
Energy: [your energy]
Energy Power: Level 0
Energy Heal: Level 6.6
Energy Tank: Level 0
```

You get **5 Conduit Tier Points** to spend on any stat **except Tank**.

## Capabilities

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Tank & Charge">
    Your tank holds **Energy Points (EP)**. Because the energies are so intense, upgrades are small, but each one adds **Energy Specials**.

    | Level | Tank | Energy Specials |
    |--|--|--|
    | 0 (start) | 100 EP | 2 |
    | 1 | 110 EP | 3 |
    | 2 | 120 EP | 4 |
    | Max | 130 EP | 5 |

    **Charge (passive):** your body slowly generates energy, **+10 EP per turn** you don't use your powers, in or out of combat. Each Charge upgrade adds **+10**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Power">
    How much damage your energy does. **17 levels**, one Defense Tier ahead of Elementals.

    | Defense Tier | Level: up to **C** | Level: **whole tier** |
    |--|--|--|
    | 3 | 0 (start) | 0.1 |
    | 4 | 1 | 1.1 |
    | 5 | 2 | 2.2 |
    | 6 | 3 | 3.3 |
    | 7 | 4 | 4.4 |
    | 8 | 5 | 5.5 |
    | 9 | 6 | 6.6 |
    | 10 | 7 | 7.7 |
    | 20 | — | X |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Heal">
    What you can **heal** while your tank regenerates, on yourself and others. The lower the level, the better.

    | Injury | Self | Others |
    |--|--|--|
    | NL-Class 3 or below | 6.6 (start) | 6 |
    | NL-Class 2 or below | 5.5 | 5 |
    | NL-Class 1 or below | 4.4 | 4 |
    | L-Class 3 or below | 3.3 | 3 |
    | L-Class 2 or below | 2.2 | 2 |
    | L-Class 1 or below | 1.1 | 1 |

    **Healing only happens while your tank regenerates**, so you have to spend energy first. It applies **every 2 turns** of regeneration until you're fully healed.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
