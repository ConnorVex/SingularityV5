**Bio-Conduits** don't manipulate anything around them. Their **own bodies** are enhanced beyond any other Conduit, which makes them one of the **best** classes.

**Starting stats:** Bio-Efficiency **Level 0** · **0** Conduit Tier Points

## Specialized Physical Evolution

:::caution[Training doesn't work]
**No amount of physical training** raises a Bio-Conduit's physical stats.
:::

Instead, you grow by **absorbing evolution items**. Each one gives Physical Points:

| Item | Base gain | Per Efficiency Node |
|--|--|--|
| Blast Shard | +3 | +5 |
| Blast Core | +5 | +5 |
| Core Relay | +10 | +5 |

When you absorb an item, you choose to either **spend the energy on your stats**, or **upgrade that item's Energy Efficiency Node** so future absorptions give more.

:::
