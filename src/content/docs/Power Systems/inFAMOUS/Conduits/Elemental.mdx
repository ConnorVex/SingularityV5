The **most common** class. Elementals **produce, manipulate and control** a natural or artificial element, from neon to concrete to **video**.

## Starting Stats

```
Element: [your element]
Elemental Drain: Level 5
Elemental Heal: Level 6.6
Elemental Power: Level 0
Elemental Tank: Level 0
```

You get **4 Conduit Tier Points** to spend on any stat **except Tank**.

## Capabilities

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Tank">
    How many **Elemental Points (EP)** you can store before recharging. Upgraded through the skill tree.

    | Level | Tank | Elemental Specials |
    |--|--|--|
    | 0 (start) | 60 EP | 2 |
    | 1 | 90 EP | 3 |
    | 2 | 120 EP | 4 |
    | 3 | 150 EP | 5 |
    | Max | 180 EP | 6 |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Power">
    How much damage your element does: who can or can't shrug it off. **17 levels.**

    | Defense Tier | Level: up to **C** | Level: **whole tier** |
    |--|--|--|
    | 2 | 0 (start) | 0.1 |
    | 3 | 1 | 1.1 |
    | 4 | 2 | 2.2 |
    | 5 | 3 | 3.3 |
    | 6 | 4 | 4.4 |
    | 7 | 5 | 5.5 |
    | 8 | 6 | 6.6 |
    | 9 | 7 | 7.7 |
    | 14 | — | X |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Drain">
    **Refill your tank** by holding out your hand and willing your element in. It's an instinct, not a skill.

    | Level | EP per source | Turns |
    |--|--|--|
    | 5 (start) | +10 | 5 |
    | 4 | +20 | 4 |
    | 3 | +30 | 3 |
    | 2 | +40 | 2 |
    | 1 | **Full tank** | 1 |

    :::caution[While draining]
    You **can't defend yourself**. Have an ally cover you, or find an opening. If you're **interrupted**:

    | Interrupted on | EP gained | Cooldown |
    |--|--|--|
    | Turn 1 | None | 5 turns |
    | Turn 2 | ¼ | 4 turns |
    | Turn 3+ | ½ | 3 turns |
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Heal">
    What draining can **heal**, on yourself and others. The lower the level, the better.

    | Injury | Self | Others |
    |--|--|--|
    | NL-Class 3 or below | 6.6 (start) | 6 |
    | NL-Class 2 or below | 5.5 | 5 |
    | NL-Class 1 or below | 4.4 | 4 |
    | L-Class 3 or below | 3.3 | 3 |
    | L-Class 2 or below | 2.2 | 2 |
    | L-Class 1 or below | 1.1 | 1 |

    **When healing happens:** on the **3rd** drain turn with a 5-turn drain, the **2nd** with a 4-turn drain, and the **1st** with anything faster.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Skills

| Skill | Class | Cost | Effect |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Elemental Blast** | <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | 30 EP | Ranged blasts of your element, **continuous or machine-gun** style |
| **Elemental Dash** | <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | 15-turn cooldown | For one turn, **become your element** for a unique property, e.g. more **speed** or **durability** (your choice) |
| **Elemental Special** | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | 1 Special | **3× your Elemental Power.** Start with **2**, **+1 per Tank upgrade**. Used Specials come back when you **refill your tank** with Drain |
