**Conduits** (or **Meta-Humans**) are a human sub-species with the **Conduit Gene**, which grants special abilities. They seem to come from a world before the Singularity and now live in both the **Mainland and the Outworld**.

Conduits **look like ordinary humans**. Spotting one takes special equipment or a special sense.

## Becoming a Conduit

Conduits are **born, not made**. The gene is a rare DNA mutation.

To become one later, you must be born a **dormant Conduit**, or have the gene **run in your family**. Without either, it's **impossible**.
