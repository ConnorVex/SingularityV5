Even **stronger than Energy Conduits**. Psychics use **telekinesis** and receive **visions**, and the most dangerous ones use **mind control**.

- **No fuel** and **no recharge**, but using powers too long **damages you**.
- Has the shared Conduit perks, but **no extra perks** in exchange for its power.

**Starting stats:** Psychic Power **Phase 5** · **0** Conduit Tier Points

## Psychic Phases

Your **Psychic Power** comes in phases. You start at **Phase 5** and want out of it fast.

| Phase | Telekinesis lifts | Takeover time | Victim resists commands | Victim breaks free |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| **5** (start) | <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength **+1** | 5 turns | Every **3** commands · `1d60` **30+** | Every RP · `1d100` **50+** |
| **4** | Strength **+2** | 4 turns | Every **6** commands · `1d60` **40+** | Every **2** RPs · `1d100` **60+** |
| **3** | Strength **+4** | 3 turns (**2** if unconscious) | Every **9** commands · `1d60` **55+** | Every **6** RPs · `1d100` **90+** |
| **2** | Strength **+6** | 2 turns (**instant** if unconscious) | <Badge text="Can't" variant="danger" /> | <Badge text="Can't" variant="danger" /> |
| **X** | Strength **+20** (that's a building!) | **Instant** on contact | <Badge text="Can't" variant="danger" /> | <Badge text="Can't" variant="danger" /> |

**Phase X** is rare, with massive requirements, and you'll need the big guns to stop one. It can also take over **Bio-Conduits and Psychic Conduits** of a **lower phase**.

## Mental Domination

Mind control: take over an **OC, Canon or NPC** and make them **serve you without question**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Requirements" icon="list-format">
    - **Prolonged contact** with you or a **medium substance** (like "Black Tar"). See takeover time above
    - Target has **no Will Manipulation** and **no Psychic abilities**
    - Target is **not** a Bio-Enhanced, Psychic, Meta or Quantum Conduit (Phase X can take Bio and lower-phase Psychics)

    Upgrades can **loosen** these requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Using your puppet" icon="star">
    **Completely up to you.** As long as your Psychic Power holds them, they can't really resist.

    Puppets also get a **slight ARC Sense**, so you can send them to **find Blast Shards, Cores and Relays** for you.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Limitations

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Keep calm" icon="warning">
    Psychics need a **calm, centered mind**. If you get **angry**, roll `1d50`: **30+** = **destabilized**.

    Destabilized: **no powers** and **incapacitated for 2 turns**, and so is **everyone you control**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mental attacks" icon="error">
    You're **more resilient**, but **not immune**. The best way to beat a Psychic is to turn their strengths against them.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
