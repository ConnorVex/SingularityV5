**Devils** (悪魔 *Akuma*) are demons from **Hell** that embody a **human fear** and draw strength from it. **Any feared concept** can become a Devil.

- **The more feared the concept, the stronger the Devil.** Death? Terrifying. A spray bottle? Not so much.
- The Singularity didn't weaken Devils. They've only gotten **stronger**.

## Shared Capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fear Empowerment" icon="rocket">
    Devils grow stronger as **they or their concept** becomes more feared, and **weaker** if that fear fades. Stay feared to stay strong. See [Devil Evolution](/SingularityV5/power-systems/chainsaw-man/devils/devil-evolution/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Corpse Possession" icon="random">
    In life-or-death situations, possess a corpse to become a [Fiend or Hybrid](/SingularityV5/power-systems/chainsaw-man/devils/fiends-and-hybrids/). **Fiends are far more likely**: Devils think taking a human body is **disgusting**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reduced Form" icon="information">
    Instead of a corpse, shrink into a **small, docile form** (usually an animal) with **greatly reduced** power. Avoid danger, recover, then return to your true form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reincarnation" icon="star">
    Devils **can't be fully killed** unless their fear is erased from the world. When one dies, fear of its concept **dips** and it can't respawn. Once the dip ends it can come back, anywhere from **a few months to hundreds of years** later.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Blood & Regeneration

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Blood Tank" icon="approve-check">
    Blood Points (BP) are your **regeneration power** and your <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />**Stamina**. Keep full to last longer.

    - Each class has its own **starting tank** and **max upgrade** value (table below). **Mundane** is lowest, **Primal** highest.
    - Upgrade it with [Fear Points](/SingularityV5/power-systems/chainsaw-man/devils/devil-evolution/).

    | Drink from | Gain |
    |--|--|
    | NPC | 10 BP |
    | OC / Canon | 25 BP |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Regeneration" icon="warning">
    **Cost: 160 BP.** Regenerate **nearly anything**, even if you're missing much of your body, as long as you're **alive** to drink.

    Dead Devils can't drink, so they **can't revive**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Devil Ability Power

**Devil Ability Power (DAP)** is the **damage value of a Devil's powers**. It works like your physical **Power** stat, but for attacks that use the Devil's concept (fire from the Fire Devil, curses from the Curse Devil, and so on).

- Each class starts at its own DAP (table below), and it rises when you go through **Metamorphosis**.
- **Fiends** have their DAP **halved**. **Hybrids** use their contracted Devil's **full** DAP.
