**Contracts** (契約 *Keiyaku*) are **verbal agreements** between a Devil and a human. The human gets access to the Devil's powers **for a price**.

:::caution[Negotiate!]
**Each Devil sets its own price.** Okay perks might cost nearly everything, and great perks might cost just a finger. If you can't negotiate, expect to be **taken advantage of**.
:::

## Contract Types

| Type | How it works | Example |
|--|--|--|
| **Summoning** | Summon the Devil after a **specific action** to fight for you. It can **refuse** if it fears the enemy | Himeno's **Ghost Devil** |
| **Supernatural Object** | The Devil lives **inside an object**. Do something specific with the object to summon it | Aki's **Curse Devil** |
| **Hybrid** <Badge text="Extremely rare" variant="danger" /> | Devil and human **merge** into a Devil-human hybrid. Few Devils will give up their life for a human's | Denji's **Chainsaw Devil** |
| **Devil Transformation** | A more intense Hybrid contract. The human gains a **monstrous Devil-like form**, unique to them (two people with the same contract get different forms). How easily they change back is **unknown** | Yuko's **Justice Devil** |

## Making a Contract

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Rules" icon="list-format">
    - Made between **two separate RPers**: one plays the Devil, one the human.
    - Must be **one of the 4 types** above.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every contract must include" icon="approve-check">
    1. **A price** (can be anything)
    2. **What the human can do** with the Devil
    3. **A punishment** for breaking it

    Missing **even one** = **no contract**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
