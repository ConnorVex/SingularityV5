## Fiend: Partial Transformation

When **mortally injured**, a Fiend turns into a mix of its Devil and human forms: **mostly animalistic**, but it can still recognize people and speak.

It stays that way until it's **killed** or fed enough blood. **A full blood tank** is needed to change back.
