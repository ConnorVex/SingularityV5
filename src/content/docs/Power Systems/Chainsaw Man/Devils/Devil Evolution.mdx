Devils get stronger as their concept is **feared more**. To **harvest fear**, make sure your concept is **associated with you**.

## Earning Fear Points

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Mentions & Manifestations" icon="star">
    | Method | Fear Points |
    |--|--|
    | **Mentioned** in an RP | 5 each, **max 10 per RP** |
    | **Manifestation:** show up at an incident tied to your concept (orchestrated or not), like the Fire Devil at a fire | 25 |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Acts of Terror" icon="warning">
    | Act | Fear Points |
    |--|--|
    | General destruction | 10 per session |
    | Kill an **E–D** class hero/vigilante | 15 |
    | Kill a **C–B** class hero/vigilante | 30 |
    | Kill an **A** class hero/vigilante | 100 |
    | Kill an **S** class hero/vigilante | 1,000 |
    | Kill **The Symbol of Peace** | 100,000 |
    | Kill **The Symbol of Freedom** | 100,000 |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Blood Tank | Cost | Regeneration | Cost |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | +10 | 10 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| 2 | +10 | 15 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| 3 | +10 | 15 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| 4 | +10 | 20 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| 5 | +10 | 20 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| 6 | +30 | 20 FP | −10 BP | 20 FP |
| **Max** | Tank becomes your **class max** ([see classes](/SingularityV5/power-systems/chainsaw-man/devils/devil-basics/#devil-classes)), then **+20 per 30 FP forever** | — | **−50 BP** | 50 FP |

*Regeneration upgrades lower the BP cost of Regeneration (160 BP at the start, 50 BP when maxed).*

## Metamorphosis

As your concept grows in fear, you can **move up a Devil class**.

- Handled by **Game Masters**, who'll notify you when it happens.
- Takes **3 real-life days**.
- You get **brand-new physical stats** and **stronger powers**.
