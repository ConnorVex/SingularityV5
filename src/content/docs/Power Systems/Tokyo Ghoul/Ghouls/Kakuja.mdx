A **Kakuja** (赫者, "red one") is an extremely rare Ghoul whose Kagune mutates into **armor around the body**. Because the mutation varies so much, Kakuja are the **most dangerous and unpredictable** Ghouls on the Mainland.

## Mutated Rc Cells

Kakuja come from **repeatedly eating other Ghouls**. When you eat a Ghoul:

1. You gain **half their Rc** (see [Feeding](/SingularityV5/power-systems/tokyo-ghoul/ghouls/ghoul-basics/#feeding)).
2. **Half of that** becomes **Mutated Rc (M-Rc)** in your kakuhou.

*Example:* eat a Ghoul with 800 Rc and you gain 400 Rc, of which **200 is M-Rc**.

:::caution
M-Rc can still be **spent on your Kagune**, so be careful not to burn through it.
:::

## Stages

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Incomplete Kakuja" icon="warning">
    <Badge text="500 M-Rc" variant="caution" />

    Your Kagune loses its type and becomes a partial Kakuja, usually covering **part of the body**.

    **Deployed:** **+10 Sub-Tiers** to Strength, Speed, Defense and Stamina.

    :::danger[Insanity]
    You **lose all reason** and attack everything in your path, unless you have the **Ghoulish Affinity** trait.
    :::
  </Card>
  <Card title="Complete Kakuja" icon="star">
    <Badge text="1,000 M-Rc" variant="danger" />

    A full Kakuja shaped by your **psyche**: anything from full armor to a **giant centipede**. An **unstoppable monster**.

    **Deployed:** **+20 Sub-Tiers** to Strength, Speed, Defense and Stamina.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
