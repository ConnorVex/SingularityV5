**Ghouls** (喰種 *Gūru*) evolved alongside humans throughout the Mainland's history. They come from a world where they were far more common, but their numbers have **dwindled**.

They're **predators** with a taste for **human flesh**, and can be violent or calm and cunning. They look **identical to humans**, so they blend in perfectly.

## Rc Cells

**Red Child Cells** (Rc細胞, *Rc saibō*) are in every living being on Terra, most of all in Mainland humans and **Ghouls**. They look like a curled-up fetus. Only Ghouls can use them, as both **fuel and weapons**.

### Rc Tank

How many Rc Cells your **kakuhou** can hold. You start with **460**. Upgrades are bought with **Ghoul Evolution Points (GEP)**:

| Earn GEP by | GEP |
|--|--|
| Combat or training where you **deploy your Kagune** | 3 per session |
| **Fully consuming** an OC/Canon | 5 |

| Upgrades | Size each | Cost each |
|--|--|--|
| 1 – 3 | +50 | 5 GEP |
| 4 – 6 | +100 | 10 GEP |
| 7 – 9 | +150 | 15 GEP |
| 10 – 12 | +200 | 20 GEP |
| **All 12** | **+1,500 (to 1,960)** | **150 GEP** |

### Feeding

| Eat | Rc gained |
|--|--|
| NPC *(ask a Game Master to play them)* | 100 |
| An OC/Canon's **limb** | 200 per limb |
| A **whole** OC/Canon | 600 |
| Another Ghoul *(non-NPC)* | **Half** their current Rc |

- **Your teeth use your physical Power stat.** You can't bite anyone whose <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense is higher than your <InlineIcon name="stats/power" />Power.
- Anything over your tank size is **lost**.

## Healing Factor

Heals **NL-Class 5 to L-Class 1** injuries **automatically** the moment you're hurt, which makes a smart Ghoul very hard to kill. It **costs Rc**:

| Injury | Rc | Injury | Rc |
|--|--|--|--|
| NL-Class 5 | 20 | L-Class 3 | 300 |
| NL-Class 4 | 40 | L-Class 2 | 380 |
| NL-Class 3 | 60 | L-Class 1 | 460 |
| NL-Class 2 | 80 | | |
| NL-Class 1 | 100 | | |

## Starvation

You lose **18 Rc every Roleplay**. Keep your supply up to stay sane.

<Steps>
1. **Below 36 Rc: Starving** <Badge text="Weakened" variant="caution" />

   Growing desperate for food. **Healing factor off**. **−2 Primary Tiers** to all stats except Intelligence.

2. **Below 18 Rc: Feeding Frenzy** <Badge text="Dangerous" variant="danger" />

   You attack **any OC or Canon nearby**, friend or not. Your stats go back to normal, then get **+3 Primary Tiers** (except Intelligence) for the frenzy.

3. **0 Rc: Death** <Badge text="Final" variant="danger" />

   If you don't eat before that Roleplay ends, your Ghoul **starves to death**.
</Steps>
