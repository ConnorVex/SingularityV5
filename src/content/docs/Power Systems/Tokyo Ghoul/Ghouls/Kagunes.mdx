A **Kagune** (赫子, "red child") is a Ghoul's **predatory organ**, used for offense and defense. Deploying it **boosts you** and creates a **weapon made of Rc Cells**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Getting your type" icon="random">
    **Request a roll** in the Rolling Channel. Some types are **restricted** and can't be rolled.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rc costs" icon="information">
    Deploying costs Rc. **Retract it intact and you get that Rc back.** You only lose Rc if it's **severed** and you have to regenerate it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

*Boosts are in Sub-Tiers.*

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Ukaku">
    **Feather-like wings from the shoulders.** Very light: boosts your speed. Weak at melee, but fires **high-speed projectiles** that move with your speed.

    **Hardening:** its Defense starts at **your Defense**, and each extra Defense Sub-Tier costs **+50 Rc**, so you can deflect attacks.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Koukaku">
    **From the shoulder blade.** The **densest** Kagune. It reshapes into **shields** that absorb damage, or **sharp weapons**.

    - **Shield Defense: 10B.** If your own Defense beats 10B, it adds **+4 Sub-Tiers** on top instead.
    - **Heavy:** the **slowest** type, and you need **9A Strength** to lift it.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Bikaku">
    **A tail from the tailbone.** The **balanced** type, for close and mid-range.

    | Matchup | |
    |--|--|
    | vs **Koukaku** | <Badge text="Strong" variant="success" /> |
    | vs **Ukaku** | <Badge text="Weak" variant="danger" /> (too fast) |
    | vs **Rinkaku** | <Badge text="Strong" variant="success" />: fast enough to power through its brute force |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Ashiaku">
    **New since the Singularity.** It comes from the tailbone like Bikaku, but **wraps around the legs**, evolved to **outrun super-speed investigators**. Ashiaku Ghouls catch prey and escape the CCG with ease.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Rinkaku">
    **Tentacles from behind the waist.** One of the **most powerful** types and great at offense. Some users form **claws or blades**. Starts with **4+ tentacles**, and more grow with enough Rc.

    - **Regen discount:** −50 Rc per tendril (only **2 Rc** if you're below 50 Rc)
    - **Strong vs Koukaku**, **weak vs Bikaku** (it can sever tendrils easily)
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Udeaku">
    **The Ashiaku's "sister," new since the Singularity.** It evolved in Ghouls who survived **super-strong CCG investigators**. Forms around the **arms** from the forearms.

    Massively boosts **lifting strength**: hurl huge objects to stop or kill pursuers and prey.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Chimera">
    **Very rare.** Can appear in the **children of two Ghouls**, combining **both parents' benefits**.

    **Cost (deploy and regen):** (mother's cost + father's cost) ÷ 2, **+200**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::tip[Special Rc Abilities]
**Requires the Ghoulish Affinity trait.** Very rarely, a Kagune develops a **special ability**, much like a Cursed Energy Property.
:::
