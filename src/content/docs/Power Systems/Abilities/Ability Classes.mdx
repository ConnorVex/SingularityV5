**Abilities** (能力, *Nōryoku*), or **super powers**, are a meta capability found among **Mainlanders**: flight, water control, shapeshifting, anything.

Thanks to genetic complexity they can be **basically anything**, from nudging an object with your mind to **rewriting the fabric of the universe**.

:::tip[Abilities vs Quirks]
Quirks **specialize**. Abilities are good at **many things**, which means more creativity and freedom.
:::

## Classes

An ability can belong to **more than one class**.

| Class | What it does | Notes |
|--|--|--|
| **Manipulation** <Badge text="Most common" variant="success" /> | Controls a **concept**: water, air, even **gravity or space** | Arguably the **most dangerous**. A smart user can be virtually unstoppable. Its only limit is your imagination |
| **Generation** (Summoning) | **Creates something from nothing**: water, fire, energy beams, or **summoned entities** | Pair with **Manipulation** to **produce and control** |
| **Transformation** | Controls the **physical structure** of your body, or someone else's: break apart into pieces, **shapeshift** into another person | Limited only by your imagination |
| **God** ("Limited") <Badge text="Not a starting class" variant="danger" /> | So powerful it's **restricted by Higher Powers**. Breaks physics: impossibilities made possible, the **purest form** of ability | **Extremely rare**, almost non-existent |
