## Metamorphosis

Like Quirks, abilities can **evolve** to tap deeper into their core.

1. **Pay AUP** to start it.
2. **Go RPs without using your ability** while it blooms into its new form.

Each Metamorphosis costs **50 AUP more** and takes **2 RPs longer** than the last:

| Metamorphosis | Cost | RPs without your ability |
|--|--|--|
| 1st | 50 AUP | 3 |
| 2nd | 100 AUP | 5 |
| 3rd | 150 AUP | 7 |
| 4th | 200 AUP | 9 |
| …and so on | +50 | +2 |

## Ability Potential

Your **Potential** decides how many upgrades you can buy before you hit your **threshold**. After that, **no more upgrades**, so pick carefully.

| Potential | Upgrades | Metamorphoses |
|--|--|--|
| Limited – Low | 5 | 1 |
| Average | 6 | 2 |
| Great – Insane | 7 | 4 |
| Immense – Colossal | 9 | 5 |
| Tremendous+ | **All** | 10 |
