  <TabItem label="Power">
    How strong your ability's **attacks and defense** are. Each level gives a value for **Standard** and **Ultimate** skills, plus the equivalent **tier**.

    | Level | Standard | Ultimate | Upgrade cost |
    |--|--|--|--|
    | No Power (start) | 0 | 0 | — |
    | Miniscule | 2 (1E) | 4 (1C) | 5 AUP |
    | Puny | 6 (1A) | 8 (2E) | 5 AUP |
    | Insignificant | 10 (2C) | 12 (2A) | 10 AUP |
    | Trivial | 14 (3F) | 16 (3C) | 10 AUP |
    | Meager | 18 (3A) | 20 (4E) | 15 AUP |
    | Little | 25 (4A) | 30 (5B) | 15 AUP |
    | Small | 35 (6C) | 40 (7D) | 20 AUP |
    | Average | 45 (8E) | 50 (8S) | 20 AUP |
    | Decent | 60 (10C) | 70 (12E) | 25 AUP |
    | Vast | 80 (13A) | 90 (15D) | 25 AUP |
    | Immense | 100 (17F) | 100 (17F) | 40 AUP |
    | <Badge text="Colossal" variant="caution" /> | 110 (18B) | 120 (20D) | 50 AUP |
    | <Badge text="Peak" variant="caution" /> | 125 (21E) | 130 (22F) | 50 AUP |
    | <Badge text="Apex" variant="danger" /> | 136 (22S) | 136 (22S) | 100 AUP |

    **Colossal, Peak and Apex** are only available with the **BIO-KING** trait.

  <TabItem label="Range">
    How far your ability reaches, and the **Speed** needed to escape it.

    | Range | Distance | Speed to escape | Upgrade cost |
    |--|--|--|--|
    | Physical (start) | Touch | 3 Sub-Tiers above your opponent | — |
    | Close | 5 – 10 m | 2A | 15 AUP |
    | Medium | 20 – 30 m | 4C | 30 AUP |
    | Long | 45 – 65 m | 6E | 45 AUP |
    | Extremely Long | 80+ m | 6C+ | 80 AUP |
  </TabItem>
