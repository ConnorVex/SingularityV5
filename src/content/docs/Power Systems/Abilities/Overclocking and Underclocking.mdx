<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Overclocking">
    Push your ability to its **absolute limit** in a tough spot: a massive power surge that can overwhelm unprepared opponents.

    **Unlock:** 100 AUP (per ability)

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="While overclocked" icon="rocket">
        - Ability Power becomes **Overclocked (115)**
        - **+3 Sub-Tiers** to <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength, <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed and <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense
        - **4 temporary Overclocked skills:** 2 Standard, 2 Ultimate
      </Card>
      <Card title="Afterwards" icon="warning">
        - Ability Power **−3 levels** for 3 turns
        - **−60 <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina**
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>

    ### Instability

    Overclocking makes your ability **unstable**, and it can **disappear forever**.

    | | |
    |--|--|
    | Each turn overclocked | **+10** Instability (out of 100) |
    | Each turn **not using** your ability | **−5** Instability |
    | At **50+** | Roll `1d10` **every turn** you stay overclocked. **1–5:** nothing happens. **6–9:** you exit Overclock, **10-turn** cooldown. **10:** your ability's genes **disintegrate**: it's **gone permanently** |
    | At **100** | **Permanent ability loss**, guaranteed |

    | Upgrade | Effect | Cost |
    |--|--|--|
    | 1 | −2 Instability per turn | 25 AUP |
    | 2 | −2 Instability per turn | 25 AUP |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Underclocking">
    **Lend power from one ability to another** that suits the situation better. Made for users with **multiple active abilities**.

    **Unlock:** 150 AUP (per ability), starting at Level 1

    | Level | Power moved | Upgrade cost |
    |--|--|--|
    | 1 | 5 | — |
    | 2 | 10 | 20 AUP |
    | 3 | 15 | 20 AUP |
    | 4 | 20 | 20 AUP |
    | 5 | 25 | 40 AUP |

    **Rules:**
    - **1:1** transfer to **one** other ability only.
    - The donor must actually **have** that much Power to spare.
    - Lasts **5 turns**, then a **10-turn cooldown**.
    - Can't be combined with **Overclocking** in either direction.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
