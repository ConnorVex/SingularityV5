Take your abilities further. Most Pyrokinetics can learn these if they meet the requirements, and **every technique must be taught** to you.

*"Upgrade Points" below means your generation's currency: SGUP or TGUP.*

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Breath of Life">
    Sense the **"life"** in an attack to tell **lethal from non-lethal** hits. Mastering it is key to understanding the Press of Death.

    Boosts your **reaction time**, so you can avoid attacks without burning much Stamina.

    :::caution
    Only **3 uses per battle**, then you **pass out**.
    :::

    | Level | Reaction time | Cost |
    |--|--|--|
    | 0 (start) | +2 Sub-Tiers | — |
    | 1 | +4 Sub-Tiers | 10 Upgrade Points |
    | 2 | +6 Sub-Tiers | 20 Upgrade Points |
    | 3 | +8 Sub-Tiers | 30 Upgrade Points |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Press of Death">
    **The Hysterical Strength of a Fire Scene.** <Badge text="Non-Pyrokinetics too" variant="tip" />

    Removes your body's limits so you can use it at **100%**, and with mastery, **beyond**.
    - Usually triggers when you're **near death**, or **manually** once taught.
    - Costs **30 <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn**.
    - **Near-death activation is always Level 0.** You can only upgrade it once taught.

    | Level | Physical stats* | Pyrokinetic levels | Cost (Pyro) | Cost (non-Pyro) |
    |--|--|--|--|--|
    | 0 (start) | +3 Sub-Tiers | +2 | — | — |
    | 1 | +6 Sub-Tiers | +4 | 10 Upgrade Points | 30 Physical Points |
    | 2 | +9 Sub-Tiers | +6 | 20 Upgrade Points | 60 Physical Points |
    | 3 | +12 Sub-Tiers | +8 | 30 Upgrade Points | 90 Physical Points |

    *\*Every physical stat except Stamina and Intelligence. Pyrokinetic levels apply to Power or Manipulation.*
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="All or Nothing">
    A Fire Soldier must be ready to **lay down everything**. Put your all into **one devastating attack**. Battle use only, once taught.

    :::danger
    It drains **all your Stamina and then some**: you **pass out** afterwards.
    :::

    **Upgrade by raising your <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina**, not with points.

    | Level | Stamina needed | Bonus |
    |--|--|--|
    | 0 (start) | 2A | +5 Power |
    | 1 | 4A | +10 Power |
    | 2 | 6A | +15 Power |
    | 3 | 8A | +20 Power |
    | 4 | 10A | +25 Power |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Crimson Techniques">
    **The Domain Expansion of Pyrokinetics.** Drawn from **extreme Pyrokinetic strength**, an apex only a few reach.

    - **8** Crimson Techniques exist, each learned from its **inventor**.
    - They **one-shot**: a hit leaves you **near death**, or **dead** if your opponent wants.

    :::note[Coming soon]
    The list of the 8 Crimson Techniques hasn't been moved over yet.
    :::
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
