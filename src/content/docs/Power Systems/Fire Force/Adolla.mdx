**Adolla** (アドラ, *Adora*), also called **Hell** (地獄, *Jigoku*), is a realm **beyond the universe's dimensions** and the source of the **Adolla Burst**. Little is known about it, but its effects are felt everywhere.

After the Singularity, Adolla went oddly **dormant** for years, until **Spontaneous Human Combustion** began. That brought back the **Special Fire Force**.

*The mysteries of Adolla await…*

## Adolla Links & Stigmas

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Adolla Link" icon="random">
    *(アドラリンク, Adora Rinku)*. Rare, and only for those who've **experienced Adolla**.

    - Connects **two people across vast distances**
    - Reveals their **emotional state**, like murderous intent
    - Can tell you when something bad has happened, like a **murder**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stigma" icon="warning">
    *(スティグマ, Sutiguma)*. A Pyrokinetic **without** an Adolla Burst who experiences a Link is left **permanently scarred**, usually on the face or **eyes**. The human mind can't handle Adolla for long.

    **No drawbacks.** **Perk:** when a Link happens nearby, you can **peer into it**: roll `1d10`, **5+** succeeds, **2 tries per Link**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Adolla Burst

**The Genesis Flame.** **Unsullied** fire from Adolla with **immense power**, found in a select few **Third Generations**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Perks" icon="star">
    - **+5 TGUP** per training session (stacks with grinding traits)
    - Can use the **entire Pyrokinetic Power tree**: past the normal **Level 15** cap to **Level Y** (and **Z** with an Adolla Grace)
    - Experience Adolla Links with **no ill effects**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pillars" icon="information">
    Most Burst holders are **Pillars**, though non-Pillars can rarely have one. What makes a Pillar is **unknown**.

    Pillars represent **emotions**. If all are brought together, they could bring about **the Great Cataclysm** once more…
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Adolla Grace & the Fourth Generation

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Adolla Entities & Grace" icon="approve-check">
    **Adolla Entities** were born in, or are deeply tied to, Adolla. They can grant an **Adolla Grace** (伝導者の加護, *Dendōsha no Kago*) to an **Adolla Burst** user through a Link.

    - A third party can also get it through a Link with someone already Graced.
    - It depends entirely on the Entity's **willingness**, and they can **revoke it** any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fourth Generation" icon="rocket">
    **Adolla Burst + Adolla Grace = Fourth Generation.** Extremely rare.

    They get a unique, theme-based ability that **breaks the laws of physics**: slowing the universe's expansion, moving **faster than light**, even **rewriting the laws of the universe**. Do not trifle with them.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
