## Spontaneous Human Combustion

**S.H.C** is the core mechanic of Fire Force. Someone suddenly **bursts into flames** for no reason. It happened in the Old World and has **started again**, though less often.

- It can happen to **anyone, at any age**.
- **90% mortality rate.** Survive it and you become a **First, Second or Third** Generation.
- **XO** isn't a natural S.H.C outcome (it's Permission Based), and the **Fourth** only comes later through Adolla.

## Generation XO

**Permission Based** hybrids with the abilities of **both Second and Third Generations**: they can **manipulate and generate** fire, making them an **immensely powerful** breed.

They have **no progression of their own**. They follow the **Second and Third Generation** paths at the same time.
