**Second Generations ("Benders")** survived S.H.C and adapted. They **control existing fire**: turn a small flame into an inferno, make little **flame puppets**, or precisely steer a **bullet's trajectory**. Their power depends heavily on their **theme**, and some flames even they can't tame.

:::tip[Cancel Third Generations]
Second Generations can **cancel a Third Generation's fire** just by willing it, as long as their **Manipulation ≥ the Third Gen's Pyrokinetic Power**. Great against rogue Third Gens.
:::

## Pyrokinetic Manipulation

Measures how much power you can **add to** or **remove from** a flame, and whether you can **cancel** Third Generations.

Values are **(Standard skill, Ultimate skill)** Power. For **defensive** Pyrokinetics, they count as an equivalent **Defense** level.
