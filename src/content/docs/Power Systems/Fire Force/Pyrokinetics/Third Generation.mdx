**Third Generations ("Spitfires")** survived S.H.C and adapted. Instead of controlling existing fire, they **produce their own** through a reaction inside the body, so they can make fire **anywhere there's oxygen**: plasma through a hilt, jets from their soles, explosions all around them.

## Oxygen

Every skill **burns oxygen** from reservoirs in your body. Run dry and keep going, and your **body becomes the fuel**: that's [Tephrosis](/SingularityV5/power-systems/fire-force/pyrokinetics/tephrosis/).

## Pyrokinetic Power

How strong the fire you produce is. Values are **(Standard skill, Ultimate skill)** Power. For **defensive** Pyrokinetics, they count as an equivalent **Defense** level.

:::caution[Level cap]
Without an [Adolla Burst](/SingularityV5/power-systems/fire-force/adolla/#adolla-burst), you **max out at Level 15**. Levels **Y** and **Z** need a Burst (and Z also needs an Adolla Grace).
:::
