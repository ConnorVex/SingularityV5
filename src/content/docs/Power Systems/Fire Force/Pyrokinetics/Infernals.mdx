**Infernals** (First Generations) are the **least desired outcome** of S.H.C. The body can't handle the power and turns into a new species, with **every cell on fire**.

- Most are in **constant agony**, reduced to destructive beings set on burning everything around them. A rare few **keep their sentience**.
- They gain **Pyrokinetic abilities** but **lose all other abilities**, though they often keep traits of their old personality.
- **No cure.** The only mercy is to put them out of their misery quickly.

> *The flame is the soul's breath. The black smoke is the soul's release. Ashes thou wert and art. May thy soul return to the great flame of fire. Látom.*

## Infernal Grades

**The lower the grade, the stronger the Infernal.** The grade is set at the moment of infernalization, and they **can't evolve**.
