**Tephrosis** (the **Ignition Limit**) is a **chronic illness** that hits Third Generations who push past their oxygen limit, so their **body becomes the fuel**. It **won't kill you**, but it **cripples** you.

:::danger[Permanent]
It starts when your oxygen goes **into the negatives**, usually from carelessness. Once it starts, there's **no stopping or curing it**. It lasts **until you die**.
:::

<Steps>
1. **Initial Overheat** <Badge text="2 RPs" variant="note" />

   You don't even notice: your abilities are quietly burning your flesh for fuel. **No debuffs**, and you can keep fighting normally.

2. **Charring** <Badge text="5 RPs" variant="caution" />

   Painful, permanent **charring** appears where your ability manifests most.
   - Physical stats **−8 Sub-Tiers**
   - Pyrokinetic Power **−4 levels**

3. **End Stage** <Badge text="Until death" variant="danger" />

   Using Pyrokinetics is nearly impossible without **immense pain**, and you need a **caretaker** to soothe your burns.
   - Reduced to the **Human Phase** of the S.T.S
   - Pyrokinetic Power drops to **Level 2**
   - **Permanent**
</Steps>

:::tip[Unique Stage: Adaptation]
**Requires the Body of Adaptation trait.** After an undefined time in the End Stage, extreme charring forms where your ability manifests, and your Pyrokinetics turn into **smoke** instead of fire. **All debuffs are removed**, and you can now use a **Tephrosis-based ability**.
:::
