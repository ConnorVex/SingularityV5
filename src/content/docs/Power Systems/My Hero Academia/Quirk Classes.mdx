**Quirks** (個性, *Kosei*) are the **genetically dominant** super power of the **Mainland**. Almost every Mainlander has at least one, awakened or not, and a person can theoretically have **infinite** Quirks. Since the Singularity, they've grown **more powerful**.

:::tip[Specialized]
Quirks are **extremely specialized**: good at **one thing, and only that one thing**.
:::

## Quirk Factors

The **genetic and physical traits** behind a Quirk, and **where it manifests** most. Quirks are rooted in biology.

*Example:* Shigaraki's **Decay** works through whatever his **five fingertips** touch, so his fingertips are his Quirk Factor.

:::danger[Protect your Quirk Factor]
If your Quirk Factor is tied to a **limb** and you **lose that limb**, you **lose your Quirk**.
:::

## Classes

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Operative (Emitter)" icon="rocket">
    <Badge text="Most common" variant="success" />

    **Generate or control** something, often by **emitting an element** (e.g. Kaminari's *Electrification*).

    - Some need an **outside power source**, like Satō's *Sugar Rush*, which needs sugar. That's what separates them from Transformation Quirks.
    - You resist **only your own** emission. Outside sources hurt you like anyone else, and your own emission can still affect you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Composite (Transformation)" icon="random">
    <Badge text="Rarest" variant="danger" />

    **Alters your own body** (rarely others'). Simple, but with a smart user one of the **best** Quirks around.

    - Needs **conscious effort** to activate, so **no accidental activations** while you're in control.
    - Can be **trained to trigger on instinct**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Heteromorphic (Mutation)" icon="star">
    A **physical mutation** from birth, from an extra eye to multiple limbs.

    - **Always on**, and can't be turned off. Only removable **physically**.
    - Mutations can have **powers of their own**, like Hawks' *Fierce Wings* (flight plus telepathic feather control).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collective (Accumulation)" icon="list-format">
    <Badge text="Sub-class" variant="note" />

    Must **gather something** before it works fully, like Deku's *One For All* gathering past users' strength.

    **Can't be the only class.** It must pair with one of the primary three: **Operative, Composite or Heteromorphic**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
