**Quirk Variants** are Quirks that have **evolved** and become far better than the original. You get one through [Quirk Instability](/SingularityV5/power-systems/my-hero-academia/quirk-evolution/#quirk-instability).

:::caution[When you get a Variant]
- **All Quirk stats reset** to the bottom, so you max them out again.
- You're **locked into that Variant**, and **Instability is gone for good**. Your Quirk has stabilized.
:::

| | <Badge text="Plus" variant="note" /> | <Badge text="Maximum" variant="caution" /> | <Badge text="Infinite" variant="danger" /> |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Rarity** | Most common | Rare, a gift from the heavens | **Rarest**: borders on being an **Ability** |
| **What it is** | Evolved from instability into a stable, **enhanced** state | Far stronger than the base **and** Plus | **Redefines the Quirk's entire purpose** |
| **Power upgrade** | Plus Var. Power (70, 74) | Maximum Var. Power (76, 80) | Infinite Var. Power (81) |
| **Extra QUP per session** | +3 | +6 | +9 |
| **Recoil** | Reduced | Greatly reduced or **removed** | **Removed** |
| **Skill cost discount** | −3 QUP | −5 QUP | −8 QUP |
| **Extra capabilities** | — | **1** that no Awakening could give | Up to **4** that no Awakening could give |
