<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Trigger">
    <Badge text="Most common" variant="success" /> The easiest Quirk drug to get. **One injection lasts 1 RP.**

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Boosts" icon="rocket">
        - **Power:** Amplified (95) (16E)
        - **Range:** Amplified (400–500 m) (Speed: 12E)
        - **2 Standard and 2 Ultimate skills** you can **only** use while on Trigger
      </Card>
      <Card title="Side effects" icon="warning">
        Roll `1d100`:

        | Roll | Effect |
        |--|--|
        | 0 – 20 | None |
        | 21 – 55 | **Weakness:** −4 Primary Tiers to <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" /><InlineIcon name="stats/speed" /><InlineIcon name="stats/defense" /> Str, Spd, Def for 3 RPs |
        | 56 – 89 | **Loss of Quirk control** for 3 RPs |
        | 90+ | **Temporary Quirk loss** for 5 RPs |
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Quirk Destroyer">
    <Badge text="Permanent" variant="danger" /> The **opposite** of Trigger.

    It targets the **Quirk Factor and Quirk Genome** and **erases them completely**. A **single injection** can remove someone's Quirk for good.

    - **Where to get it:** only on the **Black Market**, and it's **very rare**.
    - **Single use:** each dose is gone once injected.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Amplification Device">
    <Badge text="Rare" variant="caution" /> A **headset** that sells fast. If you find one on the **Black Market**, you're very lucky.

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Boosts" icon="rocket">
        - **Power:** Ultra-Amplified (110) (18B)
        - **Range:** Ultra-Amplified (500–800 m) (Speed: 13F)
        - **No side effects**, which makes it popular for a shortcut to power
      </Card>
      <Card title="Limits" icon="warning">
        - Must be **worn** the whole time. If it's removed or destroyed, your Quirk **drops back to normal** immediately.
        - **6 turns** per charge, then **2 RPs** to recharge.
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::note
Quirk drugs and devices **don't work** while your Quirk is [unstable](/SingularityV5/power-systems/my-hero-academia/quirk-evolution/#quirk-instability).
:::
