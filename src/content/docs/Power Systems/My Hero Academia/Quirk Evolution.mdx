## Quirk Awakening

A rare evolution that gives you a **deeper understanding** of your Quirk. Each Awakening grants **one** of these:
- Access to **higher Quirk Power**
- **Expanded Quirk rules**

It can happen several times until your Quirk is **maxed out**.

**Requires:**
1. **90 QUP** stored
2. An **event that helps you understand your Quirk**. It's different for everyone: a psychological push (like Toga), or a ritual (like Tenya).

## Quirk Instability

Singularity bodies are far more capable than Old World ones, leaving **"genetic space"** a Quirk can fill. Every so often, a Quirk can go **unstable** and enter a **heightened state**. That's great if it happens at the start of a fight.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Requirements" icon="list-format">
    - **Great Potential** or higher
    - Quirk Power **maxed out** (as high as you can go)
    - Quirk used in training/combat **3+ times**
    - **50 QUP** stored

    Then roll `1d1000`: **500+** = unstable. **Fail 3 times** and you must redo the last two requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="While unstable (3 RPs)" icon="warning">
    Instability lasts **3 RPs**.

    - Quirk Power becomes **Unstable (64, 68)** (11E, 11A)
    - **+2 Sub-Tiers** to <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength, <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed and <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense
    - **Quirk drugs and amplification devices don't work** on you
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Spontaneous Evolution

On the **final turn of the 3rd RP** of instability, roll `1d10000` for a chance to evolve:

| Roll | Result |
|--|--|
| 0 – 1,000 | Nothing |
| 1,001 – 5,000 | **Quirk Awakening** |
| 5,001 – 7,999 | <Badge text="Plus Variant" variant="note" /> |
| 8,000 – 9,949 | <Badge text="Maximum Variant" variant="caution" /> |
| 9,950+ | <Badge text="Infinite Variant" variant="danger" /> |

*YAGPDB is recommended for these rolls.*
