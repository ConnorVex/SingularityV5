<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Key Points" icon="star">
    - Quirks are **quick to make** and **easy to evolve**
    - Quirks are **extremely specialized**
    - Most Quirks **don't have high power potential**, with a select few exceptions
  </Card>
  <Card title="Getting Started" icon="pencil">
    ```
    Quirk Type: [Emitter, Transformation or Mutation]
    Quirk Power: No Power (0, 0)
    Quirk Range: Physical Range
    Quirk Upgrade Points: 0
    ```

    - **3 Quirk Tier Points** to spend on **Power and Range**. You **don't have to use them all**.
    - **Starting skills:** up to **3 Standard** and **2 Ultimate**.
    - **Multiple Quirks?** Each gets its **own** Tier Points, which can't be shared.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
