**Oculars** are an extremely rare trait you're born with. They **run in families** and are easy to spot, since each has a unique look.

*Some rumors say Oculars are fragments of a bigger whole. Just superstition… or is it?*

## What every Ocular gives you

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="CT Optimization" icon="rocket">
    **Passive.** Your CT's CE use is so low it's practically **Limitless Cursed Energy**.

    **Ocular Efficiency:** 0.00000000001 CEP per CT use
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enhanced CE Perception" icon="magnifier">
    Far beyond normal cursed sight:
    - Sense the **slightest disturbance** in the CE Field and find **hotspots**
    - See a small object in **4K from kilometers away**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unique Ability" icon="star">
    Every Ocular has its **own special ability** engraved into the eyes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Energy Drain" icon="warning">
    Active Oculars cost about **100 <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />Stamina per turn**. Some can be toggled; others are **always on**, which is why some users wear **blindfolds**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
