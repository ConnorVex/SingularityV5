  <TabItem label="Cursed Pests">
    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Flyheads" icon="information">
        <Badge text="Below Grade 4" variant="default" />

        The **lowest** curse there is. Even a civilian who can see CE can exorcise one.

        **In swarms**, they scramble the CE Perception of even great sorcerers, letting someone sneak up unseen.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Crawlers (Thumpers)" icon="warning">
        <Badge text="Pest, but dangerous" variant="caution" />

        A new species since the Singularity Event, like a large Old World mammal.

        - **Very slow:** outrun by someone in a wheelchair
        - **Very tough:** survives several hits from a Grade 1 sorcerer
        - Jaws **snap off limbs**, and packs can **wipe out districts**

        **Deal with any suspected Crawler immediately.**
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Finger Bearers">
    <Badge text="Special Grade" variant="danger" />

    A curse that eats one of **Ryomen Sukuna's fingers** turns into a Cursed Womb, then emerges as a Finger Bearer. **Experienced sorcerers only.**

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Can" icon="approve-check">
        - Use **Cursed Extreme** levels of CE
        - Easily wipe out groups of **Grade 3 and 2** sorcerers
        - Fire powerful **CE blasts**
        - Use the most **basic barrier techniques**
      </Card>
      <Card title="Can't" icon="error">
        - Use **Cursed Techniques**
        - Come close to Sukuna himself
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Vengeful">
    <Badge text="Usually Special Grade" variant="danger" />

    A **human who becomes a curse after death**. Rare, and they need **permission** to create unless an IRP event causes one.

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Regular Class" icon="random">
        A human who dies and is **cursed**, usually bound to someone as a **tormentor or protector** (like Rika and Yuta).

        **Sorcerer not killed with CE?** They can return as a curse:
        - Monstrous form reflecting their **human desires**
        - **Keeps** physical stats, CE Level, CT, CE skill, skills and memories
        - **Loses** any extra abilities (left with the body)
        - Automatically **Special Grade**
      </Card>
      <Card title="Imaginary Class" icon="open-book">
        Born from **collective fear** of a rumor, myth, Yokai legend or ghost story. Scare enough people and one appears. *e.g. Kuchisaki-Onna.*

        It has whatever powers **the legend says** it has.
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Death Paintings">
    **Cursed Womb: Death Paintings** are Old World **Cursed Objects** made by **Kenjaku** from curse **and** human blood. Wombs **1–3** are the most dangerous. An unknown Curse User has **made more**…

    <CardGrid>
      <Card title="Finding one" icon="magnifier">
        - Not natural: **single use**, gone forever once used
        - Whereabouts **unknown**, rumored to be on the **Black Market**
      </Card>
      <Card title="Using one" icon="warning">
        Implanted into a human, it creates a **half-human, half-curse**. Non-sorcerers can see them, and even experts struggle to tell if they're Curse Users or Cursed Spirits.

        - **Wombs 1–3:** overwrite the host with **Choso, Eso or Kechizu**
        - **Any other:** roll `1d20`, **17+** grants the Kamo Clan's **Blood Manipulation**
      </Card>
    </CardGrid>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Disaster">
    <Badge text="Above Special Grade" variant="danger" />

    Curses born from fear of **natural disasters**. Exceedingly rare, and they sit **above other Special Grades**. Only **Vengeful Cursed Spirits** rival them.
