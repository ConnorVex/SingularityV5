**Cursed Spirits** (呪霊 *Jurei*) are metaphysical beings made **entirely of Cursed Energy**. They're born where CE pools: **Cursed Energy Hotspots**, the "knots" in the Mainland's CE field. What they can do depends on their class.

:::caution[Things to keep in mind]
- Curses lack the physical functions needed for training, so they **can't physically evolve**.
- Curses can **only** use **Cursed Techniques and Cursed Energy**.
- **Vengeful Cursed Spirits** are rare and need **permission** to make unless an IRP event causes them.
:::

## Physiology

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Made of Cursed Energy" icon="information">
    Curses have blood and organs of a sort, but they're mostly negative energy.

    - **Only CE users** can see them.
    - Only **Cursed Tools** or a **sorcerer's blows** can hurt them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weakness: RCE" icon="warning">
    Positive energy destroys their body. **RCE attacks deal 4× damage** to curses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Regeneration" icon="approve-check">
    Curses **heal with plain CE**, no RCE needed, so they last longer in fights. They follow the normal Injury System.

    | Injury | Cost |
    |--|--|
    | Non-Lethal | 50 CEP each |
    | Lethal | 100 CEP each |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accelerated Growth" icon="rocket">
    A natural affinity for CE:
    - **No Potential or Intelligence requirements** (so they can use Domains)
    - **More JJUUP** and **better conversion rates**

    | | Curse rate |
    |--|--|
    | Base JJUUP gained | 10 |
    | → CEUP | 5 JJUUP → 5 CEUP |
    | → CTUP | 10 JJUUP → 5 CTUP |
    | → BTUP | 25 JJUUP → 1 BTUP |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
