## Cursed Energy Hotspots

When enough CE pools in one area, a **Hotspot** forms: a large zone where natural CE flow is greatly boosted. **This is where curses are born.**

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Using a Hotspot" icon="approve-check">
    Curses **and** sorcerers inside get **+10 CEP every 2 turns**.

    Hotspots **can run dry**. Use up every point and it dissipates as natural CE flow returns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hotspot Grades" icon="list-format">
    | Grade | CE stored |
    |--|--|
    | Small | 200 CEP |
    | Medium | 400 CEP |
    | Large | 600 CEP |
    | Immense | 800 CEP |
    | Gargantuan | 1,500 CEP |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## The Cursed Womb

Every curse starts as a **Cursed Womb** in a hotspot, slowly absorbing CE (faster over time). When it has enough, it **emerges**. Total CE absorbed sets its **Grade and CE Level**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="CE gained (real-life days)" icon="random">
    | Days as a Womb | Gain |
    |--|--|
    | Days 1 – 2 | +10 CEP / day |
    | Days 3 – 4 | +20 CEP / day |
    | Day 5 | +50 CEP |
    | Day 6+ | +100 CEP / day |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grade on emerging" icon="star">
    | CE absorbed | Grade |
    |--|--|
    | 10 – 100 | Grade 4 |
    | 101 – 200 | Grade 3 |
    | 201 – 400 | Grade 2 |
    | 401 – 699 | Grade 1 |
    | 700+ | <Badge text="Special Grade" variant="danger" /> |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
