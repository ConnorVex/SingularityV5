Cursed Tools help **sorcerers and non-sorcerers** alike fight the Cursed Spirits threatening the Mainland. Graded from **4 (worst) to 1 (best)**.

:::note[Special Grade Tools]
A tool with a **Cursed Technique engraved** in it is automatically **Special Grade** (e.g. the *Inverted Spear of Heaven*). These **can't be made** by imbuing CE: **buy one**, or take one from the cold dead hands of a sorcerer or Curse User.
:::

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Support Class" icon="information">
    Little combat use, but great for **non-sorcerers**. Usually **ordered**.

    The staple: **Cursed Glasses**, which let non-sorcerers **see Cursed Spirits** so they can fight with Weapon tools.

    **Grading up:**
    - Imbue with CE at least once in **4 fights** to reach Grade 4.
    - Each grade after needs **1 more fight** than the last (5, 6, 7…).

    A Support tool **defends** against threats of its own grade.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weapon Class" icon="rocket">
    Any weapon with enough CE poured in. You can **make these yourself**, as many as you want.

    **Imbue up to 5 CEP per turn.** Total CE imbued sets the grade:

    | Grade | CEP | Can harm |
    |--|--|--|
    | Grade 4 | 10 | Grade 4 |
    | Semi-Grade 3 | 29 | Semi-Grade 3 |
    | Grade 3 | 37 | Grade 3 |
    | Semi-Grade 2 | 44 | Semi-Grade 2 |
    | Grade 2 | 51 | Grade 2 |
    | Semi-Grade 1 | 64 | Semi-Grade 1 |
    | Grade 1 | 110 | Grade 1 + Special Grade 1 |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
