Hand Signs are seals made with your hands and fingers that boost your CE while held. On their own the boosts are **minor**. They shine with [Chants](/SingularityV5/power-systems/jujutsu-kaisen/chants/) and are mainly used to open your **Innate Domain**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Learning & using" icon="open-book">
    - **Learn one** by seeing it cast.
    - **Use one** by posting yourself performing it. Effects apply right away and last while you hold it.
    - **No penalty** if it's disrupted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom signs" icon="pencil">
    You can create a **custom Hand Sign for your Domain**, but only the signs below have special effects.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Taishakuten-in">
    *Indra, the strongest god. Confidence in your own strength.*

    **Alone:**
    - CT **+2 Grades** (max G1)
    - CT cost **−18 CEP**

    **With chants that don't require it:**
    - Chant CE penalty **−20 CEP**
    - CT penalties over 6 turns are cut to **6 turns**
    - **Conservation:** efficiency becomes **0.5** instead of 0.9
    - **Unchaining:** needs **4 words** instead of 6 · on failure, CT locked **2 turns** instead of 4
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Enma Ten-In">
    *Enma Ten, King of Hell. Giving up your humanity for power.*

    **Alone:**
    - CT cost **−10 CEP**
    - CT Range **+2 levels**
    - CT **+1 Grade** (max G1)

    **With chants that don't require it:**
    - Turn-based chant penalties are set to **exactly 6 turns** (longer ones shortened, shorter ones lengthened)
    - **Unchaining:** CT boost **+2 more** (max G1) · **no 2× CE cost** · needs **4 words** instead of 6
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Daikokuten-in">
    *Daikokuten / Shiva. Destruction and creation always follow each other.*

    **Alone:**
    - CT cost **−10 CEP**
    - CE Output **+2 levels**
    - For one turn, one CT attack at **+8 Sub-Tiers** of Speed

    **With chants that don't require it:**

    | Chant | Effect |
    |--|--|
    | Conservation | −10 CEP · only **2 words** · **1 word** per post |
    | CT Rejuvenation | −50 CEP · per-post limit **3 → 4** · failure loss −50 CEP · lockout **−6 turns** |
    | Domain Rejuvenation *(needs Extra Arms)* | −150 CEP · words **6 → 3** · Domain and chant-cost penalties only last **this CRP** |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Nyorai-In + Kujaku Myoo-in">
    *The endless cycle of birth and rebirth, and loyalty.*

    :::caution[Requires the Extra Arms trait]
    These two must be used **together**. One alone does nothing.
    :::

    **Alone:** where these really shine.
    - CT Efficiency **+3 levels**
    - CT Output **+3 levels**
    - CT Strength **+3 levels**
    - CT Range **+3 levels**

    Effects last **5 turns after** you release the sign.

    **With chants:** chant costs **−24 CEP**. That's all.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
