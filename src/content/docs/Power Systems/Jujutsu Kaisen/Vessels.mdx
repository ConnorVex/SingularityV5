**Vessels** (容器 *Yōki*) can host the **reincarnated spirit** of someone else. Rare in the Old World, now there are plenty, and many never find out they are one.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Regular Vessel" icon="information">
    The reincarnated sorcerer **takes over the body** and the original personality is **erased**, unless the sorcerer lets it stay.

    The sorcerer **keeps their original CTs**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prison Vessel" icon="approve-check">
    Has a very specific set of traits. The body **adapts** to an invading sorcerer, forcing a **Semi-Symbiotic Relationship** where the **host stays in control**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Star Plasma Vessel" icon="star">
    In the Old World, it merged with **Master Tengen** to extend their time as a human.

    The last one was **Riko Amanai**. Whether it returns, and what it's for now, is unknown.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ryomen Sukuna's Vessel" icon="warning">
    Sukuna is from the **Heian Era**, the Golden Age of Jujutsu. After the Singularity Event, his **fingers scattered** across the Mainland through space and time.

    A Vessel or Prison Vessel who **eats one** becomes Sukuna's host.

    **Current Vessel:** Nobody… for now.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
