## Unleashing Your Domain

<Steps>
1. Post your character doing a **hand sign** and saying **"Domain Expansion."**
2. **Closed, Complete Domain?** Create a **private thread** and ping everyone caught inside. *(On a forum, ask an Admin for a temporary private channel.)*
3. Post the **outer barrier forming**. This gives outsiders a fair chance at **Domain Breaking**.
4. The thread is your Domain. Shape it however you like.
</Steps>

## Domain vs Domain

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Same type" icon="random">
    *Complete vs Complete, or Incomplete vs Incomplete.* The more refined Domain wins:

    - **Higher Domain Grade wins.**
    - **Same Grade:** both roll `1d20`. Highest wins, and their Domain counts as more refined in **every later clash** with that Domain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain vs Domain Amplification" icon="magnifier">
    - **DA Grade ≥ Domain Grade:** the CT is **absorbed** and there's no auto-strike.
    - **DA Grade lower:** your DA is overwhelmed. Pray.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Incomplete vs Complete

An IDE **can never beat** a Complete Domain, but it can still help if its Grade is **equal or higher**:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Clash" icon="warning">
    The enemy's Sure Hit **doesn't apply** while you clash. The DE overtakes you after:

    | Your IDE Grade | Turns |
    |--|--|
    | Equal | 5 |
    | 1 above | 7 |
    | 2 above | 9 |
    | 3+ above | 14 |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Escape" icon="rocket">
    Open a **hole in the barrier** to escape, or to **let a stronger ally in**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## CT Burnout

After a **Complete Domain** finishes, your CT is **burnt out** and unusable for a while:

| Holder | Burnout |
|--|--|
| Normal | 35 turns |
| Barrier Prodigy | 25 turns |
| Barrier Specialist | 15 turns |
| Barrier Grandmaster *(unobtainable)* | 4 turns |

## Pure Barriers

Pure Barriers are woven into the Mainland's Cursed Energy field and **buff or nerf Domains** inside them. More may exist. The CE field fluctuates.

| Barrier | Location | Effect |
|--|--|--|
| Theta | Central Japan | **+2 levels** Domain Construction Speed |
| Alpha | Las Vegas | **−2 levels** Domain Grade |
| Gamma | South Japan | **+3 levels** Domain Range |
| Omega | France District | **All Domains are Special Grade** |
