<CardGrid>
  <Card title="New Shadow Style: Simple Domain" icon="approve-check">
    *(シン・陰流「簡易領域」 Shin Kageryū: Kan'i Ryōiki)*. The **"Domain for the Weak"**: a small barrierless domain that **nullifies Sure Hit**.

    - Any CE level, **no** Potential or Intelligence requirement
    - Easy to learn, and can be **copied by seeing it**
    - **No CT burnout**
    - Very **customizable** for offense or defense, with **unlimited Binding Vows**

    :::danger[The catch]
    Move **even an inch** from where you cast it and it **vanishes**.
    :::
  </Card>
  <Card title="Esoteric Technique: Hollow Wicker Basket" icon="star">
    *(奥義「彌虚葛籠」 Ōgi: Iyako Tsuzura)*. The ancient predecessor of Simple Domain.

    - Interlock your fingers and extend your arms to make a **woven-basket barrier** that **nullifies Sure Hit**
    - Cheap to maintain
    - **You can move** while holding the hand sign
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain Amplification" icon="random">
    Wrap yourself in an **empty Domain** that **sucks in and neutralizes** any CT it touches, including a Domain's Sure Hit.

    :::caution
    Your **own CT is off** while it's active. Fight with your fists or other abilities.
    :::
  </Card>
  <Card title="Secret Art: Falling Blossom Emotion" icon="warning">
    *(落花の情 Rakka no Jō)*. **Family exclusive.** A CE shroud that **counterattacks** the moment a Sure Hit touches it.

    Also a **sword technique**: coat your blade and anything the CE touches takes a devastating blow.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Domain Breaking

Domains can be **broken from the outside** by anyone not caught inside who is a sorcerer or has a Cursed Tool.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="By Power" icon="rocket">
    | Domain Grade | Power needed |
    |--|--|
    | Grade 4 | 20 |
    | Semi Grade 3 | 25 |
    | Grade 3 | 30 |
    | Semi Grade 2 | 55 |
    | Grade 2 | 60 |
    | Semi Grade 1 | 80 |
    | Grade 1 | 90 |
    | Special Grade | Impossible |
  </Card>
  <Card title="By Cursed Tool" icon="puzzle">
    The tool must be **1+ grade higher** than the Domain.

    **Special Grade Domains** need a specialized **SGCT**.

    :::note[Result]
    A broken Domain **vanishes instantly** and the caster's CT **burns out**, even if nothing happened inside.
    :::
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
