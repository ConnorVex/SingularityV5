Barrier Techniques are a **supreme art** of Jujutsu. Even a simple one can turn a fight in your favor.

**The catch:** they take a lot of skill and have requirements to even use. That makes them a **trump card** alongside Maximum Techniques.

## Barrier Mastery

The **hardest** and **longest** part of the JJK System to master. Stick with it and you could become a legend in Singularity.

**Conversion:** 55 JJUUP → **1 Barrier Technique Upgrade Point (BTUP)**

## Curtains

**Curtains** (帳 *Tobari*) are a simple barrier usable by Curses, Curse Users and Sorcerers. Easy to learn.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Curtain" icon="information">
    - **Hides** everything inside from non-sorcerers
    - **Traps** enemies inside
    - Goes **pitch dark** to lure out curses

    **Cast:** chant + hand sign:
    > *"Emerge from the darkness, blacker than darkness. Purify that which is impure."*

    | | |
    |--|--|
    | Cost | 10 CEP |
    | Duration | 10 turns or until broken |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Commissioned Curtain" icon="star">
    Bought on the **Black Market**. Engraved on a **talisman**: channel CE into it and say the chant.

    - **Customizable,** e.g. block a specific sorcerer, or all CE users
    - Lasts **indefinitely** until the talisman or barrier is destroyed
    - **Cuts off phone signal**: no contact from inside
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
