Techniques that change what casting a Domain means. Only the best sorcerers use them, and the requirements are steep.

| Technique | Potential | How to get it | Cost | CT Burnout |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| All or Nothing DE | Immense | Upgrade · **maxed Complete DE** | 173 CEP | <Badge text="No" variant="success" /> |
| Open Domain | Tremendous | Upgrade · **Barrier Specialist** | — | — |
| Size Alteration | Immense | IRP · **Barrier Prodigy** | — | — |
| Domain Burst | — | Taught by the **King of Curses** | 189 CEP | <Badge text="No" variant="success" /> |

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="All or Nothing Domain Expansion" icon="rocket">
    Expands, hits and vanishes in **0.2 seconds**, cast in the heat of the moment.

    - **Can't be countered** by Anti-Barrier Techniques unless they're **already up**
    - **No CT burnout**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Supreme Art: Open Domain" icon="star">
    A Domain **with no barrier**. A Binding Vow leaves an escape route, and in exchange the range becomes **Open Domain Range**: the **entire category**.

    **Vs a closed Domain:** an Open Domain's physical attack can hit the closed Domain **from outside** and **destroy it**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Special Art: Size Alteration" icon="random">
    **Shrinks your Domain's outer barrier** (the inside stays huge).

    Its use is **against Open Domains**: their attacks can't hit a tiny shell, so it **won't shatter** mid-clash. It doesn't protect against external Domains.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sacred Art: Domain Burst" icon="warning">
    Confine your Innate Domain to your **hands**, then burst it outward. **Sure Hit** applies to everything the burst touches.

    - **Bypasses Simple Domains** and other Anti-Barrier Techniques
    - **No CT burnout**
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
