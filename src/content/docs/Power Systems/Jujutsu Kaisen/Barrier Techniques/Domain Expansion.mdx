Domain Expansions are barriers built from Cursed Energy and used as a **trump card**. Without countermeasures, a Domain is basically a **guaranteed win**.

## Domain Types

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Lethal" icon="error">
    Built to **harm or kill** the target. Very hard to complete, and needs refinement even after.

    *Gojo's Infinite Void · Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine · Jogo's Coffin of the Iron Mountain · Mahito's Self-Embodiment of Perfection*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Non-Lethal" icon="approve-check">
    **Rare** today. It doesn't harm the target, so it gets a **near-instant construction time**.

    *Hakari's Idle Death Gamble · Higuruma's Deadly Sentencing*
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Incomplete vs Complete

| | Incomplete (IDE) | Complete (DE) |
|--|--|--|
| Barrier | **None**. Must be cast inside a concrete barrier, not an open area | Builds its **own** barrier from CE |
| Sure Hit | <Badge text="No" variant="danger" /> | <Badge text="Yes" variant="success" /> CT attacks can't be dodged |
| Potential | Great+ | High+ |
| Intelligence | — | Gifted |
| Cast cost | 145 CEP | 520 CEP |
| Upkeep | 10 CEP / turn | 20 CEP / turn |
| CT burnout | <Badge text="No" variant="success" /> | <Badge text="Yes" variant="danger" /> |
| Example | Megumi's *Chimera Shadow Garden* | — |

### Incomplete Domain (IDE)

The **infant stage** of a Complete Domain: your Innate Domain brought into reality, without a barrier.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Perks" icon="approve-check">
    - **+5 Sub-Tiers** to Strength, Speed and Defense
    - CT **+3 Grades** (max G1)
    - Simple Domains, HWB and other Anti-Domain measures **don't work** against it
    - Can **punch a hole** in a Complete Domain (but can't win the clash)
    - **No CT burnout** afterwards
  </Card>
  <Card title="How to get one" icon="open-book">
    - **Train:** earn Domain Upgrade Points and unlock the IDE upgrade. Slow.
    - **Witness one:** get caught inside a Complete or Incomplete Domain. Dangerous if it's a Lethal Complete Domain. Good luck!
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Complete Domain (DE)

The **pinnacle of Jujutsu**. Your CT is embedded in the barrier, so **every CT attack has a Sure Hit effect**: no amount of speed lets you dodge.

:::caution[Watch out for]
- **Anti-Barrier Techniques** can nullify the Sure Hit.
- Domains can **clash**, and the more refined one always wins.
:::

**Requirements:** Potential **High+** · Intelligence **Gifted** · **1+ Black Flash** · a **maxed** Incomplete Domain

<Steps>
1. At the end of **every RP**, roll `1d20`. On **15+**, your next RP takes place in a **Dream World**: your Innate Domain.
2. Search for your **true self** (it can look like anything you want). Roll `1d20` **3 times**, needing **17+**.
3. **Fail:** you wake up and must wait **2 RPs** before trying again.
4. **Succeed:** accept your true self, and your Complete Domain is ready.
</Steps>
