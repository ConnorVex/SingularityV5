Chants are words that sound like gibberish but are powerful tools. They're usually paired with [Hand Signs](/SingularityV5/power-systems/jujutsu-kaisen/hand-signs/) for stronger effects, but work alone too.

:::tip[Unlocking chants]
Hit a **Black Flash**, then resonate with your soul. Words will appear in your mind. It's up to you to figure out what they do.
:::

## Chanting in Combat

<Steps>
1. Get an **opening**.
2. **Channel CE** (each chant has its own cost).
3. **Begin the chant.** Each class limits how many words you can say per post.
</Steps>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="While chanting" icon="warning">
    - Once started, you **must finish**. Skipping a post = **failed chant**.
    - You **can't fight back**. Dodging is your only defense.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ways to interrupt" icon="error">
    - **Physical:** a hit to the **throat or mouth** (risky, you need to get close).
    - **Cursed Energy:** disrupting or disabling the caster's CE.
    - **Environmental:** terrain or weather changes that demand the caster's focus. This can backfire and give them room to finish.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Chant Classes

| Class | CE Cost | Words | Per Post | Effect |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| Conservation | 25 | 4 | 2 | CT costs **0.9 CEP** for 4 turns |
| Rejuvenation (CT) | 100 | 8 | 3 | Restores a **burnt-out CT** |
| Rejuvenation (Domain) | 150 | 6 | 2 | Restores your **Innate Domain** |
| Unchaining | 50 | 6 | 2 | **+2 CT Grades** for one devastating attack |
| Counter | Varies | 3–5 | 2 | Counters an opponent's chant |

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Conservation">
    The **most common** class, focused on saving CE.

    **Effect:** CT Efficiency becomes **Enchanted Efficiency**: **0.9 CEP** per use for **4 turns**.

    **Requires:** 25 CEP · 4 words · max 2 words per post

    :::danger[If you fail]
    −75 CEP · CT Efficiency drops to the **lowest level** for 4 turns
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Rejuvenation">
    **Must be used with Hand Signs.** Both versions require a technique burnt out from **both a Domain and RCT Rejuvenation**.

    | | CT Rejuvenation | Domain Rejuvenation |
    |--|--|--|
    | Effect | Restores a burnt-out CT | Restores your Innate Domain |
    | CE Cost | 100 | 150 |
    | Words | 8 | 6 |
    | Per post | 3 | 2 |
    | Hand Sign | — | **Taishakuten-in** |
    | **If you fail** | −160 CEP · CT **disabled 12 turns** | −200 CEP · **no Domain for 2 CRPs** · all chants **+130 CEP** for 2 CRPs |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Unchaining">
    Unchains your CT for **one attack** so powerful it's your trump card. Your CT's safeguards are removed, so an **AOE will hurt you too**.

    **Effect (1 turn):** CT **+2 Grades** · attack costs **2× CE** · all self-protection safeguards **lost**

    **Requires:** 50 CEP · 6 words · max 2 words per post

    :::danger[If you fail]
    −150 CEP · CT **locked for 4 turns**
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Counter">
    Counters the other three classes during an opponent's long chant. **Once per fight**, so choose wisely.

    | | Penalty Increase | Nullification | Cost Increase |
    |--|--|--|--|
    | Effect | If they fail, their CE penalty and durations are **×2** | **Undo their last 3 words**. You must stay in one place | All chants cost **+200 CEP** for the fight (**both** of you) |
    | CE Cost | 1.75× theirs | 2× theirs | 85 |
    | Words | 3 | 5 | 3 |
    | Per post | 2 | 2 | 2 |
    | Hand Sign | Daikokuten-in | Taishakuten-in | Enma Ten-In |
    | **If you fail** | −2× your CE · their penalties hit **you at ×4** for the CRP | −4× your CE · **no chants** for the CRP | −285 CEP · the increase hits **only you** · CT Efficiency **−3 levels** for the fight |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
