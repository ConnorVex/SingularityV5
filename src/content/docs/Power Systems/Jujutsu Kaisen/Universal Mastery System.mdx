The JJK System is several systems in one. Each has its **own upgrade currency**, all bought with one shared currency: **Jujutsu Universal Upgrade Points (JJUUP)**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Earning JJUUP" icon="rocket">
    **+5 JJUUP** per training session or combat where you **use Jujutsu**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Converting JJUUP" icon="random">
    | Into | Rate |
    |--|--|
    | CEUP (Cursed Energy) | 5 JJUUP → 2 CEUP |
    | CTUP (Cursed Technique) | 10 JJUUP → 2 CTUP |
    | BTUP (Barrier Technique) | 55 JJUUP → 1 BTUP |

    Other systems list their own rates on their pages.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Jujutsu Grandmasters

Grandmasters have mastered Jujutsu inside and out. There are currently **2**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Learning under one" icon="open-book">
    - Earn **more JJUUP**
    - They can **unlock upgrades for you** without you spending JJUUP
  </Card>
  <Card title="Becoming one" icon="star">
    - **Unlock and max out everything** in the Cursed Energy and Cursed Technique systems
    - Be **recognized** by an existing Grandmaster
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
