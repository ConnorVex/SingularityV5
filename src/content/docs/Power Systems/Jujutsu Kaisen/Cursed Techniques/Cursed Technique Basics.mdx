**Cursed Techniques (CTs)** are special abilities used by Jujutsu Sorcerers, Curse Users and Curses alike.

- You're **born** with one, and most people are born **without** one.
- A CT is the **80% talent** of Jujutsu: it decides a sorcerer's skill.
- Every CT is **fuelled by Cursed Energy**, and each has its own strengths and downsides.

Every CT has **1 Type** and **1 Class**.

## Types

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Innate" icon="star">
    - **Unique** to the user, often tied to their personality or desires.
    - Usually manifests around **age 5**, rarely much later.
    - **Bonded** to the user: copying one is nearly impossible without the user's skill.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inherited" icon="open-book">
    - Passed down through a **family**.
    - **Stronger** than Innates: you get a literal instruction manual.
    - **Downside:** it's well known, so opponents often know how it works.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Classes

| Class | What it does | Example |
|--|--|--|
| **General** | Grants a special ability, nothing more. Most CTs are General. | Aoi Todo's *Boogie Woogie* |
| **Shikigami** | Summons Shikigami, in forms that depend on the CT. | Megumi Fushiguro's *Ten Shadows* |
| **Support** | **Rarest.** Boosts others and **can't be used on yourself**. | Arata Nitta's *Pain Killer* |
