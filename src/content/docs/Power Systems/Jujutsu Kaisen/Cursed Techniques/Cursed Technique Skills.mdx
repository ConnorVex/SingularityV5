:::note[CTs don't evolve]
A Cursed Technique's **core concept never changes**. Skills let you **expand** on that core instead.
:::

| Skill | Class | Unlock | Bonus | CE Cost |
|--|--|--|--|--|
| Extension | <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | 10 CTUP each | — | Your CT Efficiency |
| Maximum Output | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | 15 CTUP each | **+10** CT Power, 1 turn | See below |
| Maximum | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | Upgrade locked · **Trivial+** CE | **+20** CT Power, 1 attack | See below |
| CT Reversal | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | Upgrade locked · **RCE** required | **+40** CT Power | 15 RCE |

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Extension Techniques" icon="puzzle">
    Different ways of using your CT's core concept. They **extend** its properties, giving you a range of attacks if you're creative.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Maximum Output" icon="rocket">
    Pour all the CE you can into an Extension. It **must be attached to an Extension** and can't be used alone.

    **Cost:** half your CE Level + your CT Efficiency cost.

    *Example:* Limited (100) and Subpar (22): 100 ÷ 2 = 50, then 50 + 22 = **72 CEP**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Maximum Techniques" icon="warning">
    Your CT's **strongest attack** and your trump card.

    **Cost:**
    - Common to Legendary CE: **half** your CT stores
    - Mythic and up: **400 CEP**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cursed Technique Reversal" icon="random">
    Fuel an Extension with **positive energy (RCE)** to get the **opposite** of its normal effect. Very hard to master, but levels the playing field.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## CT Mastery

CTs are much harder to master than Cursed Energy. Spend **Cursed Technique Upgrade Points (CTUP)** on the CT Skill Tree.

**Conversion:** 10 JJUUP → **2 CTUP**
