Every Cursed Technique has three stats. All start at a base value and must be **upgraded**.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Power">
    How potent your CT is. Works like your physical **Power** stat and is measured in **Grades**.

    :::note
    CT Grade is **not** the same as Sorcerer Grade.
    :::

    | Grade | Power |
    |--|--|
    | Below Grade 4 (start) | 5 |
    | Grade 4 | 15 |
    | Semi Grade 3 | 25 |
    | Grade 3 | 35 |
    | Semi Grade 2 | 40 |
    | Grade 2 | 50 |
    | Semi Grade 1 | 70 |
    | Grade 1 | 120 |
    | <Badge text="Special Grade" variant="danger" /> | 145+ |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Range">
    The **maximum** reach of your CT's effects, and the Speed needed to escape it. A CT can always use **less** than its max range.

    | Range | Distance | Speed to escape |
    |--|--|--|
    | Physical | 0 m | Combat dodge |
    | Close | 1 – 5 m | 1A |
    | Medium | 10 – 15 m | 3E |
    | Long | 25+ m | 4E+ |
    | <Badge text="Impossible" variant="caution" /> | 100 m | 6A+ |
    | <Badge text="Heavenly" variant="danger" /> | Unknown | — |
    | <Badge text="Unknown" variant="danger" /> | Unknown | — |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Efficiency">
    How much CE **each use** of your CT costs.

    :::danger[Not enough CE?]
    You can use your CT **once per fight**. Using it again **kills you**.
    :::

    | Efficiency | CEP per use |
    |--|--|
    | Extremely Poor (start) | 30 |
    | Very Poor | 29 |
    | Poor | 25 |
    | Subpar | 22 |
    | Tolerable | 20 |
    | Average | 18 |
    | Decent | 16 |
    | Adequate | 13 |
    | Great | 10 |
    | Amazing | 8 |
    | Insane | 6 |
    | Remarkable | 3 |
    | <Badge text="Permission" variant="tip" /> Unparalleled | 1 |
    | <Badge text="Permission" variant="tip" /> Terrifying | 0.5 |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
