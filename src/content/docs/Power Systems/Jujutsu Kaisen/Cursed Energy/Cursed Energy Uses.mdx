  <Card title="CE Perception" icon="magnifier">
    **Passive.** Anyone who uses CE can sense it: ominous presences, hotspots and other sorcerers. You can also **see Cursed Techniques**.

    :::caution
    If you **don't** use Cursed Energy, you can't defend against Cursed Techniques. They're a **guaranteed hit**.
    :::
  </Card>
  <Card title="CE Enhancements" icon="approve-check">
    Channel CE into an aura for **Temporary Physical Tiering Points (TPTP)**. Each TPTP moves a stat up **1 Sub-Tier**.

    | | Cost |
    |--|--|
    | 1 TPTP | 10 CEP |
    | Upkeep | 5 CEP / turn |
    | Extra upkeep | +10 CEP / turn per 5 TPTP |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Divergent Fist

**Divergent Fist** (逕庭拳 *Keitei Ken*), first used by Yuji Itadori. Your fist moves faster than your CE, so the CE lands a moment later: **two blows in one punch**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="When it happens" icon="information">
    It's **involuntary**. It happens when:
    - Speed is **4A+**, and
    - CE Control is **Level 1**

    It **disappears** once your CE Control levels up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="How it hits" icon="rocket">
    - Second blow: your Power stat **+10 Power**
    - Takes **1 turn** to come out
    - **Slower** than your opponent: you get hit
    - **Same speed:** both roll `1d50`, highest wins
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

:::tip[Mastering Divergent Fist]
With the **Sorcerer Supreme** trait, spend **25 CEUP** to use it on command. Also requires **CE Control Level 5**.
:::
