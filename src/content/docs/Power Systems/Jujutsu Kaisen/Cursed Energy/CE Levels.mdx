Your Cursed Energy is measured in **Cursed Energy Points (CEP)**. Once rolled, your level is **permanent**.

## Rolling Your Level

<Steps>
1. **Roll your class:** `/roll 1d1260`

   | Roll | Class | Chance |
   |--|--|--|
   | 0 – 510 | <Badge text="Common" variant="default" /> | 40.5% |
   | 511 – 930 | <Badge text="Uncommon" variant="success" /> | 33.3% |
   | 931 – 1190 | <Badge text="Rare" variant="note" /> | 20.6% |
   | 1191 – 1250 | <Badge text="Legendary" variant="caution" /> | 4.8% |
   | 1251 – 1260 | <Badge text="Mythical" variant="danger" /> | 0.8% |

2. **Roll your amount** using that class's roll command below.
</Steps>

## Recharge

Cursed Energy isn't infinite. It refills in two ways:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="RP to RP Recharge" icon="random">
    A small top-up at the **start of every new RP**.

    | Level | Gain |
    |--|--|
    | 0 (start) | +2 CEP |
    | 1 | +4 CEP |
    | 2 | +6 CEP |
    | 3 | +8 CEP |
    | 4 | +10 CEP |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Complete Recharge" icon="warning">
    Needed when you **fully drain** your tank. Starts when that RP ends. RP to RP Recharge is **off** until it's done.

    | Level | Tank refills after |
    |--|--|
    | 0 (start) | 5 RPs |
    | 1 | 4 RPs |
    | 2 | 3 RPs |
    | 3 | 2 RPs |
    | 4 | 1 RP |
    | X | Next RP |
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
