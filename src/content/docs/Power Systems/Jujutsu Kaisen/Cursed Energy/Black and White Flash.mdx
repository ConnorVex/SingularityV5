## Black Flash

**Black Flash** (黒閃 *Kokusen*) is a distortion in space caused by applying CE to a physical hit within **0.000001 seconds**. Your CE flashes back and you run at **120% CE efficiency**.

It's **not random**: there's no roll. You unlock it through the **Cursed Energy Skill Tree** and trigger it by meeting requirements IRP.

<Steps>
1. **Meet all of these:**
   - Black Flash skill unlocked
   - In a **CRP** against another individual
   - Fight has lasted **4+ turns**
   - You used CE in **2 of those 4** turns

2. **Land consecutive hits** based on your CE Control:

   | CE Control | Hits needed |
   |--|--|
   | Level 0–1 | 9 |
   | Level 2 | 6 |
   | Level 3 | 5 |
   | Level 4 | 4 |
   | Level 5 | 3 |
   | *Blessed By The Sparks* trait | 1 |

3. **Make your next attack a Cursed Energy attack.** It **auto-hits as a Black Flash**. If it isn't a CE attack, start over from step 1.
</Steps>

### Effects

Stat boosts apply to all stats **except Intelligence and Stamina**.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="First Black Flash ever">
    - **+1 Primary Tier and +4 Sub-Tiers**
    - **+40 CEP** restored
    - CE Output temporarily **maxed**
    - CT Efficiency temporarily **maxed**
    - CT Grade **+3** (max Grade 1)
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Every Black Flash after">
    - **+4 Sub-Tiers**
    - **+20 CEP** restored
    - CE Output **+2 levels**
    - CT Efficiency **+2 levels**
    - CT Grade **+2** (max Grade 1)
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

### The Zone

A Black Flash puts you in **the Zone** for **3 turns**, giving you a deeper understanding of CE.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Zone Black Flash" icon="star">
    Cheaper than a normal Black Flash. You need to:
    - Be on the **3rd (final) Zone turn**
    - **Not have been hit** during the Zone

    Then your next turn can be a Black Flash, giving **3 more Zone turns**. Repeat as long as you keep meeting the requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Getting hit" icon="warning">
    Getting hit by **anything** during the Zone cancels the Zone Black Flash. The effects fade after the 3rd turn.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## White Flash

**White Flash** (*Shirosen*) is a newer phenomenon, like Black Flash but powered by **Reverse Cursed Energy**. You're going **against** the flow of CE, so there's **no Zone**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Requirements" icon="list-format">
    - Black Flash unlocked
    - RCE unlocked
    - **Maxed** Cursed Energy Tree
    - Been in the Zone at least once
    - Hit a Black Flash while in the Zone
  </Card>
  <Card title="Triggering it" icon="rocket">
    - Fight someone **2+ entire phases above you**
    - Throw everything away (your life, others' lives, your dreams) and reach true **inner enlightenment**
    - Imbue a strike with **RCE**: it lands as a White Flash
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Effects (6 turns)

- RCE flows through your body: **RCT applies automatically** and heals your wounds.
- **CE-based targeting barely works** on you, and **Domains kick you out**.
- All your CE becomes RCE, so you can **only use Cursed Technique Reversals**.
- When it ends, you must wait for your **CE to recharge**.
