**Cursed Energy** (呪力 *Juryoku*) is negative energy born from emotions like anger, rage and sadness.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Where it comes from" icon="information">
    - Anything that feels emotions has Cursed Energy and feeds the surrounding **Cursed Energy Field**, willingly or not.
    - Found in both **The Outworld** and **The Mainland**, but only **Mainlanders** can use it to its fullest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="What it's for" icon="rocket">
    - Has supernatural properties and many uses.
    - Its main job is fuelling **Cursed Techniques**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Cursed Energy Properties

Most people's Cursed Energy is uniform. A rare few have CE with a special **property**, like Hajime Kashimo's Electric Property.

:::danger[Why properties are dangerous]
A property lets your Cursed Energy **bypass all Cursed Energy Defense**.
:::

**Getting one:**
- Your Cursed Technique must be **entirely dependent on something you can't easily access**. Properties show up most in techniques that can't work without a unique condition.
- Meeting that is a **chance, not a guarantee**. It's decided on submission.
