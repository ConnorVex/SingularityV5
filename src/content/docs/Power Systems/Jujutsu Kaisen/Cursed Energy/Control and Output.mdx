## Cursed Energy Control

You start with little to no control. Neglecting it locks you out of parts of Jujutsu and hurts you in combat.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Readable attacks" icon="magnifier">
    Opponents can **read your attacks** from your CE flow before you throw them. In some situations this allows **Auto-Dodging**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CE Leakage" icon="warning">
    At **Stunted+** CE, you leak **10 CEP per turn** of combat or training.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

| Level | Effect |
|--|--|
| 0 (start) | Full leakage, readable attacks |
| 1 | −2 CEP leakage |
| 2 | −2 CEP leakage · opponents **can't read** your attacks |
| 3 | −2 CEP leakage · unlocks **CE Re-Enforcement** skill |
| 4 | −2 CEP leakage |
| 5 | **No more leakage** · can use **Barrier Techniques** (if plausible) |

## Cursed Energy Output

Output is how much CE you can unleash at once to **boost your attacks**. Each boosted attack **spends** the CEP listed. More CE lets you reach higher output.

| Output | CEP Spent | Power Bonus |
|--|--|--|
| None (start) | — | — |
| Extremely Poor | 5 | +0.1 |
| Poor | 10 | +0.5 |
| Miniscule | 15 | +1 |
| Small | 20 | +2 |
| Average | 25 | +3 |
| Above Average | 30 | +4 |
| Good | 35 | +5 |
| High | 40 | +6 |
| Amazing | 45 | +7 |
| Extremely High | 50 | +8 |
| Intense | 55 | +9 |
| Immense | 60 | +10 |
| Maximum | 100 | +15 |
