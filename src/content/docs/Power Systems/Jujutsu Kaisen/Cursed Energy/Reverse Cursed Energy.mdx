**Reverse Cursed Energy (RCE)** is positive energy made by multiplying Cursed Energy by Cursed Energy. Extremely rare and hard to produce. Only a handful of sorcerers can use it.

It fuels two techniques: **Cursed Technique Reversal** and **Reverse Cursed Technique**.

## Unlocking RCE

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The slow way: learn it" icon="open-book">
    Follow the **Skill Tree** upgrades. Tedious and slow, but safe.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The fast way: nearly die" icon="warning">
    In a **Near Death Experience**, roll `/roll 1d100`.

    - **98+:** you unlock RCE and use RCT to heal yourself.
    - **Anything else:** say hi to god for us.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Producing RCE

RCE isn't automatic. You **convert** it yourself.

| | |
|--|--|
| Conversion | **10 CEP → 1 RCE Point** |
| Decay | After **2 RPs**: −10 RCE · then **−5 RCE every RP** |

## Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT)

RCT uses RCE to **heal injuries**, which normal CE can't do. Healing **others** requires a **Special Trait**.

| Injury | On yourself | On others |
|--|--|--|
| Non-Lethal | 10 RCE | 20 RCE |
| Lethal | 15 RCE | 30 RCE |

*Costs are per injury.*

## Technique Restoration

*Unlocked through the CE Skill Tree.* If your CT burns out after a **Domain**, destroy the part of your brain holding it with CE and rebuild it with RCT so you can keep using it.

The first time always works. After that, roll `/roll 1d100` each time:

| Attempt | Success on |
|--|--|
| 2nd | 0 – 98 |
| 3rd | 0 – 84 |
| 4th | 0 – 42 |
| 5th | 0 – 21 |
| 6th | 0 – 10 |
| 7th | 0 – 2 |
| 8th | 0 – 1 |
| 9th+ | 0 |

:::danger[On a failed roll]
Nosebleed, and you're **stunned for 1 turn**. Your opponent gets **1 free auto-hit**.
:::
