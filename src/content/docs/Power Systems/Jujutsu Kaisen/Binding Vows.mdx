**Binding Vows** (縛り *Shibari*) are CE contracts made with yourself or with others. **Keep** the vow and you gain power. **Break** it and you'll regret it.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Vow on Yourself" icon="star">
    - **Riskier is better:** CE comes from negative energy, so the more a vow **endangers you**, the bigger the payoff.
    - **Payoff:** a temporary boost to **CE Output, Strength and efficiency**.
    - **Example, "Limiting":** restrict the CE you can use for a while, then get a power boost to overwhelm your enemy.
    - **Breaking it:** not serious. You just **lose the gains**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vow with Others" icon="random">
    - Made between **two parties** and bound with CE.
    - Only valid if the terms **favor both sides**.
    - **Breaking it:** an **extremely dangerous** consequence. Nobody knows what.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
