**Cyberpsychosis** is a rare but deadly set of mental disorders caused by **too much Cyberware**, often triggered by existing mental illness. Victims lose touch with reality and humanity, chrome up compulsively, then go on a **killing spree**.

:::danger[Terminal]
**No cure.** The MCDC classifies it as **terminal**, and the only fix is a **bullet to the head**. Peaceful **euthanasia** is offered to those who want to go before it sets in.
:::

## Rolling for It

Thanks to the Singularity, Mainland bodies handle far more chrome, so it's **not guaranteed**. You roll when installing Cyberware, based on your mental state:

| Your background | Roll | Cyberpsychosis on | When to roll |
|--|--|--|--|
| Normal upbringing, **no trauma** | `1d10000` | **9900+** | Every **3** pieces installed |
| **Traumatic** upbringing | `1d10000` | **5000+** | **Every** piece installed |

## Immunosuppressants

The body always attacks Cyberware as foreign. **Immunosuppressants** stop that to protect the implant.

They also **pause Cyberpsychosis for 2 RPs**. After **5 pauses**, they stop working.

## Stages

<Steps>
1. **Chrome Junkie** <Badge text="8 RPs" variant="note" />

   Obsessed with getting more Cyberware. **On every 2nd RP, you must get chrome.** Skip it and it's **applied automatically**.

2. **Hallucinations** <Badge text="4 RPs" variant="caution" />

   The body attacks your neural-ware, causing hallucinations, weakness and sometimes **paralysis**:
   - **Hallucinations every 2 turns**
   - **−2 Sub-Tiers** to all stats except <InlineIcon name="stats/intelligence" />Intelligence **every 6 turns**

3. **KILL EVERYTHING!!!!** <Badge text="Until killed" variant="danger" />

   You get your strength back, plus an uncontrollable urge to **massacre every living thing**:
   - **+2 Primary Tiers and +4 Sub-Tiers** to all stats except Intelligence
   - You **rampage until you're killed**
</Steps>
