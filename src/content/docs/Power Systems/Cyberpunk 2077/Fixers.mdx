**Fixers** are middlemen between **clients and mercs**, matching the right merc to the right job. Want quick cash? Talk to a Fixer.

## Street Cred

Your **reputation** in the merc world. More Street Cred = **better-paying jobs**, which are usually **more dangerous**.

| Street Cred Level | Cred per level | Jobs unlocked |
|--|--|--|
| 1 – 10 | 1,000 | <Badge text="Low Risk" variant="success" /> |
| 11 – 20 | 1,500 | <Badge text="Medium Risk" variant="note" /> |
| 21 – 30 | 2,000 | <Badge text="High Risk" variant="caution" /> |
| 31+ | 2,500 | <Badge text="Extreme Risk" variant="danger" /> |
