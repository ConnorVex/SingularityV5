**Cyberware** (cybernetic implants) is any mechanical augmentation of the body using bio-technology. Once strictly medical, it went commercial, and now people use it to shape their bodies into their **"perfect" selves**.

## Slots

Each piece of Cyberware takes a slot. You start with **12**. Slots marked <Badge text="Upgradeable" variant="tip" /> can be expanded in the [Skill Tree](/SingularityV5/power-systems/cyberpunk-2077/mastery-system/).

## Ripperdocs

**Ripperdocs** install Cyberware for you, for a fee. You'll find their clinics all over the Mainland.

## Grades

| Grade | Where to get it | What it offers |
|--|--|--|
| <Badge text="Civilian" variant="default" /> | **Every** Ripperdoc. Cheap | Only the base benefits of its class |
| <Badge text="Military" variant="caution" /> | **Few** Ripperdocs stock it | Built for **combat**. Far better than Civilian, with extra perks like faster use or more damage |
| <Badge text="Modified" variant="danger" /> | **Can't be bought**: scavenge it or make it | Pushed to the limit by Cyberware geniuses, with perks **unique to the modder**. Making your own needs **Genius Intelligence** |

## Tiers

Higher tiers unlock at Ripperdocs as you **level up** (see [Mastery System](/SingularityV5/power-systems/cyberpunk-2077/mastery-system/)).

| Tier | Level needed |
|--|--|
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 15 |
| 4 | 25 |
| 5 | 50 |
