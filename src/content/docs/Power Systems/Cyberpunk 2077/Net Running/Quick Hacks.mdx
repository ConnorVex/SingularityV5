**Windup** = turns before it takes effect. **Duration** = how long it lasts. **CD** = cooldown.

## Offensive

| Hack | RAM | Windup | Duration | CD | Effect |
|--|--|--|--|--|--|
| **Blinded** | 2 | 2 | 2 | 4 | **Cyber-optics only.** Shuts off their optics: they're **blind** |
| **Static Shock** | 3 | 5 | 1 | 8 | Electrical surge through their Cyberware: **stun**, giving you an **Auto-Hit** |
| **Optical Flashbang** | 5 | 5 | 2 | 10 | **Multi-target.** Overloads audio and optical implants to **disorient** them, for a free **Auto-Hit** or an escape. If they're **jacked into the net**, their allies get it too |
| **System Overload** | 8 | 5 | KO 4 | 15 | Fries their systems and **knocks them out**. Won't kill them |
| **OS Hack** | 10 to start | 7 | — | 15 | Breaks into their **Operating System**, then spend more RAM on an option below |
