**Net Runners** modify themselves with Cyberware to **hack their way out of situations**, or run special **Operating Systems** that boost their bodies.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Operating Systems (OS)" icon="setting">
    A program in every Cyberware user's **neural net**. Your OS decides how you interact with the world: hacking, enhanced perception, slowing time, and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="RAM" icon="rocket">
    The **currency** for Quick Hacks.

    - Start with **4 RAM**. Buy **RAM Upgrades** at Ripperdocs.
    - **Recovery:** 1 RAM bar every **15 turns**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quick Hacking" icon="random">
    Hacking **on the move**, without Full Diving, to get an edge. Affects the **environment, equipment, and anyone with Cyberware**. Your OS changes which hacks you get and how well they work.

    **Limit:** 1 Quick Hack per person at a time (upgradeable).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full Diving" icon="warning">
    For **experienced** Net Runners. Special equipment and Cyberware let you **enter the internet** as a virtual world, full of opportunities and **dangers**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
