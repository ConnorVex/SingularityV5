## Intrusions

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Bank Accounts" icon="star">
    Most banks use the same encryption, so firewalls shouldn't stop you. Roll `1d10000`:

    | Roll | Result |
    |--|--|
    | 0 – 5,000 | **Fail** |
    | 5,001 – 7,000 | Success, **unsecure** line |
    | 7,001+ | Success, **secure** line |

    A **secure line** keeps less experienced Net Runners out of your intrusion.

    **Then:** ping the account owner with how much you're stealing. They **must transfer it**. Big amounts might mean **retaliation**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Corporate Documents" icon="open-book">
    Attack a **corporation's** net and breach several **firewalls**.

    - Each firewall gives you **3 rolls**. The thresholds are set when you start.
    - **Fail 3 times:** locked out for **2 RPs**.
    - Failed breaches can alert **anti-Net Running systems** or **guard Net Runners**.

    **Get in** and you can access their **unsecured documents and files**. Try to make a profit.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Hazards

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Viruses" icon="warning">
    Malicious neural code that attacks your system. Most are easy to spot, but they slowly damage your neural net until you **flatline**.

    You **can't remove them yourself**. Get scanned and cleaned at a **Ripperdoc**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hostile Net Runners" icon="error">
    Likely to show up whether you're hacking or just roaming. They try to **siphon your info**.

    | Attacker | To deflect |
    |--|--|
    | Regular | `1d100` ×3, **80+** on **2** rolls |
    | **Corpo** | `1d100` ×3, **90+** on **all 3** |

    **You can be the hostile one too.** Against another OC/Canon holder, you both roll `1d100` and **highest wins**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
