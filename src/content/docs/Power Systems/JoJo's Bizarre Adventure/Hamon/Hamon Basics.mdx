**The Ripple** (波紋 *Hamon*) is the energy of **Sendō** (仙道, "Way of the Hermit"), an ancient martial art. Special breathing creates energy that works **like the Sun**. It can slow aging, and it's deadly in combat.

:::note[Who can use it]
Learnable in **the Mainland and the Outworld**, but only **Outworlders** can **start** with Hamon.
:::

## The Hamon Gauge

Hamon Points (HP) are your fuel for Hamon Techniques, like Cursed Energy Points in JJK.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Breathing Post" icon="random">
    To charge Hamon in combat, make a **Breathing Post**:
    1. At least **4 sentences** long
    2. **No attacking** or other actions during it

    You gain HP equal to your **Charge Rate**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drain" icon="warning">
    Hamon needs **constant breathing**. Every turn you go without a Breathing Post, you **lose HP** equal to your **Drain** rate.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Starting Stats

```
Hamon Gauge: 0/200
Hamon Drain: 50 HP per post
Hamon Charge Rate: 50 HP per Breathing Post
Hamon Mastery: 0
```

## Drawbacks

| Weakness | Effect |
|--|--|
| **Iron & steel** | Too strong for Hamon to affect |
| **No breathing** | Gauge drains fast |
| **NL-Class 3 to NL-Class 1 injury** | **−100** max Gauge and **−50** Charge Rate |
| **L-Class 3+ injury** | Hamon is **completely lost**: it needs strong blood flow |

## Hamon Techniques

Hamon's combat applications come in skill classes. You start with the **Starter** skills, then must be **taught** the rest or learn them yourself. See [Hamon Skills](/SingularityV5/power-systems/jojos-bizarre-adventure/hamon/hamon-skills/).

Hamon Points can also be traded for <InlineIcon name="stats/stamina" />**Stamina**: 10 HP → 5 Stamina.
