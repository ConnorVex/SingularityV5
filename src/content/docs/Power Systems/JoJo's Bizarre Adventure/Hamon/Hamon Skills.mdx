<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Starter">
    *"You just started learning, don't expect to know a lot right away."*

    Both are **passive** and come with Hamon.

    | Skill | Effect |
    |--|--|
    | **Hamon Enhancements** | At **50+ HP**: **+5 Sub-Tiers** to <InlineIcon name="stats/strength" />Strength, <InlineIcon name="stats/speed" />Speed and <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense. Trade **10 HP → 5 Stamina**. |
    | **Poison Resistance** | At **50+ HP**, most toxins are burnt out of your body. Stronger poisons need more HP. |
  </TabItem>

    | **Ripple Hair Attack** | <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | Varies | Hardens hair. Defense: 10 HP **1A** · 20 HP **2A** · 30 HP **4A** · 40 HP **5A** · 50 HP **7A** (max) |

    | **Overdrive** | <Badge text="Standard" variant="note" /> | 25 HP | Big Ripple pulse through an object: **AOE**, **+10 Power**. Base of other Overdrives |
    | **Sendō Wave Kick** | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | 83 HP | Knee strike: instant **L-Class 2 injury** to Vampires/Undead |
    | **Rebuff Overdrive** | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | 76 HP | Elbow strike: **+15 Power** |
    | **Scarlet Overdrive** | <Badge text="Ultimate" variant="caution" /> | 60 HP | Flaming Ripple: **+15 Power**, sets the target on fire for **3 turns** (**18 damage/turn**). Flame can move to other body parts |
