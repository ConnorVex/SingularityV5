**Vampires** (吸血鬼 *Kyūketsuki*) were once human, turned into **undead** by a **Stone Mask**. They thrive in shadow and are weak to the sun.

:::danger[This is a race]
Think carefully. Once you become a Vampire, **there's no turning back**.
:::

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Blood Gauge" icon="random">
    Vampires don't charge up. They **drink blood**. Draining NPCs or characters gives **Blood Points (BP)**, filling a gauge of up to **100 BP**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Techniques" icon="open-book">
    - **Starter** skills come automatically when you turn.
    - **Advanced** skills (all **Ultimate**) must be **self-taught**. Your only master is yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Strengths" icon="approve-check">
    Powers that **enhance your body**. **Semi-immortal**: you stay in your prime forever unless killed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weaknesses" icon="warning">
    Turning **reverses your natural energy flow**. All natural energy follows the Sun, so **Hamon and sunlight** are now deadly to you.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Mastery

Earn **4 Vampire Upgrade Points** per **combat or training** session where you use your vampiric abilities.

:::note[Skill tree coming soon]
The Vampire Skill Tree (including Space Ripper Stingy Eyes and Vaporization Freezing upgrades) hasn't been moved over yet.
:::
