A **Stand** (スタンド *Sutando*) is a projection of your **soul**, the physical form of your **fighting spirit**. It can do things you can't. Stands are **very rare**.

## Gaining a Stand

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Natural" icon="star">
    Most Stand users are **born** with an active Stand. It awakens somewhere between **ages 5 and 16**, depending on environment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stand Arrow" icon="warning">
    Not born with one? Get pierced by a **Stand Arrow**, laced with a virus.

    - **Find one:** roll `1d1000` in your RP. **700+** = Stand Arrow. Once per RP.
    - **Survive:** with enough **will and fighting spirit**, you gain your Stand. Without it, the virus is **100% lethal**.
    - Your fate is **rolled by the Game Masters**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What Stands Can Do

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Form" icon="information">
    - Usually appears **standing near you**, with **superhuman** physical ability
    - Can be **humanoid, non-humanoid, an object, or a phenomenon**
    - Only **Stand users** can see Stands, and only **Stands can hurt Stands** *(except when they don't)*
    - Usually **no consciousness**, mirroring your desires. **Some act independently**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Esoteric Ability" icon="rocket">
    Every Stand has a **wild ability**, unique or shared, that reflects your soul: from making fire, to **reverting entropy**, to **controlling time**. Useful in combat **and** everyday life.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Drawbacks

| Drawback | Effect |
|--|--|
| **Weak will** | Without enough willpower and fighting spirit, you lose control. Your Stand **turns on you** and you sicken and **die** |
| **Shared damage** | Damage to your Stand **hits you too** *(except when it doesn't)*. Lose its hand, lose yours. A lethal hit to it will likely kill you, and a dying user means a very weak Stand |
| **Range** | Every Stand has a **max range**. **Close-range** Stands are usually strong melee fighters, **long-range** ones are weaker with other uses |
