## Creating Your Stand

You start with **15 Stand Points** to spend on stats. Submit them as **plain text** or with the **Stand Stat Wheel** website.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Stand Stat Wheel" icon="external">
    Use the [Stand Stat Wheel Creator](https://spooky-ghostwriter.tumblr.com/jojo/standmaker).

    - **Set every stat to None** before adding points.
    - A **maxed** stat = **S Rank**.
    - A stat needs **at least 1 point** to be valid.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stand Points" icon="star">
    The currency for upgrading your Stand. Spend it on:
    - A **new Stand skill**
    - **Better stats**
    - **Awakening** your Stand's engraved ability
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Stand Gauges

| | Stand Awakening | Stand Mastery |
|--|--|--|
| Gauge | **0/180** | **0/110** |
| Fills by | Stand Points | **+3** each time your Stand is used in combat/training |
| When full | **Awakens** your engraved ability, unlocks its **ability slot**, and **+4 points** to every stat except Dev. Potential | Pick one: a **new Stand skill**, put the points toward **Awakening**, or **+2 points** to any stat except Dev. Potential. Then it **resets to 0** |

You can **move points** between Mastery and Awakening either way.

## Developmental Potential

How much **room for growth** your Stand has. You choose it with your starting points. Growing **drains** it, and those points **never come back**. At **Mastered / None**, you've hit the wall and **can't evolve further**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Grades" icon="list-format">
    | Grade | Potential | Points |
    |--|--|--|
    | E | Little to none | 5 |
    | D | Low | 10 |
    | C | Medium-Low | 20 |
    | B | Medium | 25 |
    | A | Medium-High | 30 |
    | S | High | 40 |
    | ∞ | Infinite | ∞ |
  </Card>
  <Card title="Point Drain" icon="warning">
    | Action | Cost |
    |--|--|
    | New move | −5 |
    | Stand Awakening | −20 |

    Awakening with **under 20** left sets Dev. Potential straight to **Mastered**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
