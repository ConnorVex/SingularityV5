| | Evolved | Requiem | ACT |
|--|--|--|--|
| **How** | Pierced by a Stand Arrow a **second time** | Pierced by a **Requiem Arrow** with a **clear goal** in mind | **Low Potential** trait only |
| **Requires** | An **already awakened** ability, and the arrow must find you **worthy** | Find a Requiem Arrow. Said to appear **once the doors to Heaven open** | — |
| **Gain** | A **second ability** reflecting your awakened one, plus **6 stat points** | A unique, abstract, **very** powerful ability. **S Rank in all stats** while active | The Stand **metamorphoses** as you mature, usually stopping at **ACT 3 or 4**. **+3 points** per ACT (any stat except Dev. Potential) |
| **Duration** | <Badge text="Permanent" variant="success" /> | <Badge text="Temporary" variant="caution" /> Reverts when you **win or lose**, then **4 RP cooldown** | <Badge text="Permanent" variant="success" /> |

:::note[ACT Stands]
ACT Stands replace the **Awakening** stat with a **Metamorphosis** stat that progresses at the same rate.
:::
