Earn **2 Spin Upgrade Points** each time you use Spin in **combat or training**. It's a hard style to master.

:::caution[No trait bonuses]
Spin is the **only** mastery system where traits that **increase gains don't apply**.
:::

| Upgrade | Spin Gauge | Destructive Enhancement | Shielding |
|--|--|--|--|
| 1 | +10 | +5 | +1 Sub-Tier |
| 2 | +10 | +10 | +1 Sub-Tier |
| 3 | +20 | +15 | +1 Sub-Tier |
| 4 | +20 | — | +1 Primary Tier |
| 5 | +30 | — | +2 Primary Tiers |
| 6 | +30 | — | — |
| **Max** | **+50** | **+20** | **+3 Primary + 2 Sub-Tiers** |

:::
