**The Spin** (回転 *Kaiten*) is a supernatural phenomenon of **near-perfect rotation**.

:::note[Who can use it]
Only **taught in the Outworld**, since Outworlders understand nature's design better, but **anyone can learn** it. Only those with a **specific trait** can **start** with Spin.
:::

## The Spin Gauge

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Charging" icon="random">
    Post your character **charging Spin** into their medium: **+25 Spin Charge** per charge post, up to **100**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spending" icon="warning">
    Using Spin Techniques **spends charge**, and you lose **25 per post**. Keep it charged!
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

**Starting stats:** Gauge **0/100** · Gain **25** per charge post · Loss **25** per post

## Drawbacks

- You need an object that **can hold Spin**. Not everything works, and finding one in a pinch can be hard.
- **Golden Spin** needs a **Golden Rectangle** in the environment, or it **can't be used**.
- **Cavalry Spin** is hard to set up: keep your horse at a steady pace with **no obstacles**, or start over.

## Spin Techniques

Taught by **Spin Masters**. Some teach only what you need now, others more. It depends on how you approach them.

| Technique | Cost | Effect |
|--|--|--|
| **Imbuement** | — | Imbue Spin into anything that rotates on an axis (balls, corks, even fingernails). This **charges your gauge** |
| **Damage Enhancement** | — | More Spin = harder hits. **25–50:** 25 Striking Power · **60–80:** 50 · **100+:** 75 |
| **Projectile Control** | — | Steer a launched object's path, and have it **return to you** |
| **Golden Skin** | 20 / turn | **Full gauge required.** Coat your body for **+6 Sub-Tiers** <InlineIcon name="stats/defense" />Defense while maintained |
| **Limb Flattening** | 10 per limb | **Steel Ball required.** Harmlessly flatten limbs to squeeze through gaps. Breaking concentration cancels it early |

:::tip[Custom Techniques]
Master **every** Spin Technique, including **Golden and Cavalry Spin**, to become a **Spin Master** and create your own.
:::
