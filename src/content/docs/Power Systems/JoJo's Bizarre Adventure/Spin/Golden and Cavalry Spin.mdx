## Learning Cavalry Spin

<Steps>
1. Be **told** that Cavalry Spin exists. You gain a new stat: **Enlightenment 0/250**.
2. Use Spin **while riding a healthy horse**: **+2 Enlightenment** each time.
3. At **250**, you see the perfect rotation in your horse's movement and unlock Cavalry Spin.
</Steps>

**To charge it:** max Spin charge + Golden Spin active + ride **3 turns uninterrupted** at a steady pace.

## Ball Breaker

A special Stand for **Standless** users who **fully master Spin** and own a **Zeppeli Steel Ball**.

- **Summoned by:** using Cavalry Spin and throwing a Golden Spin–imbued Zeppeli Steel Ball.
- **Weak physically,** but its abilities more than make up for it:
  - Bypasses most **dimension-based** moves
  - Moves in **Stand Time Stops**
  - Its punches cause **rapid aging** to the target **and** their Stand
