The **Ultimate Life Form** is the final stage of **Vampire evolution**. Thanks to changes in Terra's humans, **any Vampire** can reach it. The power is intoxicating and takes the **strongest will** to control.

## Becoming the Ultimate Life Form

<Steps>
1. **Max out** your physical stats, your Vampire abilities, and your Stand (if you have one).
2. Get a **Stone Mask (Variant)** that can hold the Red Stone. Find it **by chance**, or have someone with the **Crafter** trait forge it.
3. Find the **Red Stone of Aja**, a stone brimming with the Sun's power.
4. Put the Red Stone in the mask's receptacle and **wait for sunrise**. Expect people to **try to stop you**. Be ready to fight.
5. When the sun shines through the Red Stone, you've **achieved perfection**.
</Steps>

## Perks

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Maxed Stats" icon="rocket">
    All stats become **20A** instantly, with no harm.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mind" icon="star">
    - <InlineIcon name="stats/intelligence" />Intelligence becomes **Super Genius**
    - Potential becomes **Unparalleled**
  </Card>
  <Card title="Biology Manipulation" icon="random">
    Your body holds every DNA sample within you:
    - Change any body part (gills, wings…)
    - Turn limbs into **sapient, loyal animals**
    - Alter **other** living beings' bodies
  </Card>
  <Card title="Survival" icon="approve-check">
    - **Reactive Evolution:** adapt to anything, from volcano cores to space (*Body of Adaptation* trait)
    - **Hyper Regeneration:** regrow limbs and survive decapitation, so injuries barely matter
    - **Nigh-Absolute Immortality:** no need to eat, sleep or breathe, and **immune to the sun**
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Ultimate Hamon

Only Ultimate Life Forms can use it. You gain it **instantly**, with **every Hamon skill unlocked**.

- **Breathing is optional:** your body generates it passively, always available.
- Every Ultimate Hamon skill hits with **155 Power**.
